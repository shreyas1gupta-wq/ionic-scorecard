# Xorlog — China Comparables Research
Status: DONE
Last updated: 2026-07-16

Scope: China's retail-investing app landscape as a comparables set for Xorlog — Xueqiu (social investing), East Money (retail super-app), Tonghuashun (screener/terminal + AI), Futu/moomoo and Tiger Brokers (modern cross-border UX), plus the CSRC regulatory frame vs India's SEBI RA/IA regime. Xorlog is NOT a broker and gives no buy/sell advice pre-license — the regulatory column matters as much as the feature column here.

## AI/LLM features across the cohort (state of play, 2024-2026)

- **Every major Chinese platform now runs its own finance LLM, and all of them ground it in proprietary data, none sell it standalone**: Tonghuashun's HithinkGPT/Wencai (the most mature — NL screening predates LLMs by a decade), East Money's Miaoxiang (in-app assistant), TigerGPT (DeepSeek-R1-powered, free in-app), Moomoo AI (Q&A + stock analysis + announcement summaries, bundled free in Japan). AI is a retention/differentiation layer that deepens the funnel — monetization stays in brokerage/data/fund fees. Consistent with Xorlog's thesis: charge for data+orchestration, not for "the AI."
- **Regulatory pre-clearance of AI features is normal there**: HithinkGPT needed CAC generative-AI filing before launch; TigerGPT went through an SFC review before its Hong Kong release. India has no equivalent gate yet (SEBI's Jun-2025 AI/ML consultation is the precursor) — Xorlog gets to ship BYOK AI without a filing regime today, but should design as if one is coming.
- **The dominant AI use-cases shipped**: NL screening (Wencai), announcement/earnings-call summarization (all four), ticker Q&A grounded in the platform's database (all four), sentiment digests (TigerGPT). Notably ABSENT even in China: AI emitting portfolio-level buy/sell verdicts — the platforms stop at analysis/summarization, the same line Xorlog draws pre-RA (and CSRC's Jun-2026 announcement confirms verdict-emitting AI is where enforcement will land).

## CSRC vs SEBI — the regulatory boundary Xorlog actually cares about

**China's licensing regime (functional analogue of SEBI RA/IA, but stricter and frozen):**
- Legal basis: Securities Law + 1997 《证券、期货投资咨询管理暂行办法》 (Interim Measures on Securities & Futures Investment Consulting). Giving securities investment advice to the public for compensation requires a **证券投资咨询 (securities investment consulting) licence**; individuals practising under it need 2+ years securities experience + a qualification exam (SAC-administered). Research reports may only be published by licensed securities companies/subsidiaries holding the consulting qualification.
- **The licence pool is effectively FROZEN: CSRC stopped accepting new securities-investment-consulting licence applications around 2016** (narrow exceptions incl. HK/Macau cross-border arrangements). China's equivalent of "just go register as an RA" does not exist — you buy/partner with one of the ~80 grandfathered licence-holders. India's SEBI RA regime (₹15k + NISM exam, open applications) is dramatically MORE accessible — an underappreciated India advantage for Xorlog's Phase-2 plan.
- **Software counts as advice**: CSRC treats 荐股软件 (stock-recommendation software — screeners/signal tools that emit buy/sell outputs) as licensed investment-consulting activity (2012-13 crackdown forced registration or shutdown). This is the direct read-across for Xorlog: China already litigated the "is a scoring/screening tool advice?" question and answered YES when the tool emits recommendations — same substance-over-form logic SEBI applies (Trendlyne registering Windmill Capital). Neutral data/tools stay outside; verdict-emitting tools are inside.
- **Community discussion is regulated at the CONTENT layer, not the securities layer**: Guba/Xueqiu discussion is legal without a securities licence, but platforms carry liability for illegal content under CAC (internet regulator) rules. **Oct-2025 CAC credentialing rules: influencers publishing on finance (also medicine/law/education) must hold professional credentials; platforms (Douyin/Weibo/Bilibili) must enforce, fines to ¥100k; millions of finfluencer accounts frozen/under review.** China now requires a credential to even TALK about finance at scale — well beyond SEBI's Jan-2025 finfluencer circular (which bans regulated entities from associating with unregistered finfluencers but doesn't credential speech itself).
- **AI-generated research has a dedicated dual-gate**: any public generative-AI service needs CAC filing under the 2023 Interim Measures for Generative AI (Tonghuashun's HithinkGPT was the first finance LLM through it); AND at the June-2026 Lujiazui Forum CSRC chairman Wu Qing announced coming guidance on AI in capital markets, explicitly targeting **illegal AI-generated stock recommendations**, AI-driven rumor-spreading, and AI-theme stock hype ("strictly investigate and punish"). China's posture: AI output that looks like a stock recommendation is treated as a recommendation — the tool's licence status, not the human/AI authorship, decides legality. SEBI's Jun-2025 AI/ML consultation paper points the same way; Xorlog's rule that BYOK AI must not emit templated per-stock verdicts pre-RA matches BOTH regulators' direction of travel.
- **Enforcement is real, retroactive and personal**: Futu ~¥1.85B and Tiger ~¥410M fined/confiscated (May-2026) for YEARS-earlier unlicensed cross-border brokerage, CEOs personally fined ¥1.25M each, 2-year rectification with mainland accounts restricted; Shanghai influencer "Xu" criminally charged for touting an unauthorized ETF-options app via Weibo/livestreams; finance commentator Wu Xiaobo silenced on Weibo. Directly comparable in kind (if larger in scale) to SEBI's Asmita Patel (₹53.67cr impounded) and Avadhut Sathe (₹546cr) actions.

**One-line comparison:** China regulates the SPEECH layer harder (credentialed finfluencers, CAC content liability, frozen advice-licence pool, AI filings) while India regulates the ADVICE layer harder relative to access (open RA registration but Dec-2024 amendment sweeping model portfolios/track records in). Both regulators share the substance-over-disclaimer doctrine; both fine founders personally. The Chinese platforms that thrived inside the perimeter (East Money, Tonghuashun) did it by ACQUIRING licences onto an audience built with neutral content/tools — the exact sequencing Xorlog has planned. The ones that ignored the perimeter (Futu/Tiger at home) lost their home market retroactively.

## Synthesis — what's genuinely importable to an India platform

- **The East Money sequencing is the master pattern: free content/community/tools → massive habitual audience → acquire the licence → monetize regulated products at >50% net margins.** Every durable Chinese winner (East Money, Tonghuashun, Xueqiu) ran unregulated-but-neutral layers for years first; Xorlog's Phase 0→2 plan replicates this deliberately, and India's open RA regime makes the licence step far easier than China's frozen pool.
- **Natural-language screening (Wencai) is proven mass-retail behavior at 100M+-user scale, a decade before LLMs** — the highest-conviction feature import in this set: NL query on top of Xorlog's neutral screener, with LLMs now making it near-free to build where Tonghuashun needed years of proprietary NLP.
- **Platform-verified credibility beats claimed credibility**: Xueqiu's timestamped portfolio rebalance broadcasts and Futu's broker-verified position badges are the two best community mechanics found — Xorlog's journal-verified-trade badge (Phase 3) is the same mechanic with the journal as verifier, and pre-RA it must attach to strategies/frameworks rather than named-stock portfolios (Dec-2024 SEBI amendment).
- **China already answered "is a screener advice?" — yes, the moment it emits verdicts**: recommendation-emitting software requires the advice licence there, mirroring SEBI's substance test; plus both CSRC (Jun-2026 Lujiazui) and SEBI (Jun-2025 consultation) are converging on treating AI-generated stock recommendations as regulated advice regardless of authorship. Xorlog's neutral-tool/user-weighted-framework line pre-RA, and its no-templated-AI-verdicts rule, sit on the right side of BOTH regimes — keep them.
- **Regulatory perimeter risk is retroactive and personal** (Futu/Tiger fined billions years later, CEOs personally; SEBI impounding crores similarly) — and internationalization was the survivors' hedge. Build compliance-by-design now, and keep the codebase exportable (multi-market data model, English-first) as the cheap Plan-B option.

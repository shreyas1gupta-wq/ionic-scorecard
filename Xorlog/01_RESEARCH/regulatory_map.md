# Xorlog — Regulatory Map (SEBI / India Securities Law)
Status: COMPLETE (first pass). Date: 2026-07-16.
Scope: Where exactly the SEBI-licence line falls for a non-broker fintech offering trade journaling, screener, backtesting/strategy-builder, BYO-key AI research, a future paid research/recommendations plan, and API-based execution helpers.

Labeling: **[DATA]** = sourced from a fetched primary/secondary source with URL. **[INTERPRETATION]** = reasoned reading of the rules, not a direct quote — verify with a securities lawyer before launch. **[UNVERIFIED]** = claim from a secondary source not independently corroborated, or conflicting figures found across sources.

**This document is regulatory research, NOT legal advice. Every [INTERPRETATION]-tagged conclusion — and ideally the whole roadmap — must be checked with a SEBI-qualified securities lawyer before Xorlog ships the corresponding feature. Nothing here should be quoted externally as a legal opinion.**

Primary legal basis throughout: SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, as last amended 10-Feb-2025 (via the Third Amendment Regs notified 16-Dec-2024) [DATA sebi.gov.in]; SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013; Guidelines for Research Analysts circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025 dated 8-Jan-2025; RA FAQs circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/105 dated 23-Jul-2025; algo-trading circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated 4-Feb-2025.

## The Line

| Feature | Licence needed? | Basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trade journaling** — user logs their own trades privately, no output shared with/sold to others | None | Not a "research report," recommendation, or advice to any client — pure personal record-keeping. No SEBI regime addresses journaling software at all. | HIGH |
| **Screener** — quantitative filters/scans over public data, NO Xorlog-authored buy/sell/hold label attached to a specific security | None | RA Regs exemption list explicitly covers "giving statistical summaries of the financial data," "presenting financial data...in an easy to understand manner," and "technical analysis relating to demand and supply in a sector or index" [DATA cskruti.com exemptions summary]. Matches real-world precedent: Screener.in and Chartink appear to operate unregistered on exactly this theory. | HIGH |
| **Screener that surfaces a buy/sell/hold flag or price target on a named security** (even auto-generated, even "for education") | RA registration | RA Regs cover "any buy/sell/hold recommendation...based on technical analysis for a specific security" — sector/index-level technical views are exempt, single-stock calls are not [DATA taxguru.in FAQ analysis]. This is the single most important line inside "screener" as a feature — Xorlog must keep its screener at the sector/index/factor level or a neutral-metric level, never emit a first-party "BUY" verdict on one ticker. | HIGH |
| **Backtesting/strategy-builder tool** — user configures their own rules, tool shows historical performance of the USER's own strategy, no signal broadcast to other users, no auto-execution | None (as a pure computational tool) | No SEBI provision regulates backtesting software per se; it is analogous to a spreadsheet/calculator. Precedent: AlgoTest, TradingView Pine Script, Quantsapp's non-advisory scanner features operate without RA licence for the pure-tool layer. | MEDIUM-HIGH [INTERPRETATION — no direct SEBI ruling on backtesting tools exists; reasoning by analogy to the "tool vs advice" exemption pattern] |
| **Strategy marketplace** — users publish/sell/subscribe to each other's strategies with implied or stated performance, esp. if marketed as "assured"/"consistent" returns | High enforcement risk regardless of registration; strategy AUTHOR may need RA if effectively selling signals for consideration | Direct precedent: SEBI's Sept-2022 circular required brokers to disassociate from any algo platform referencing past/expected returns of hosted strategies; March-2026 adjudication orders fined 10 brokers ₹16 lakh total for continuing to deal with Tradetron past that deadline [DATA moneylife.in, sebi.gov.in enforcement order]. The platform's own "neutral tech provider" disclaimer did not stop SEBI from going after the BROKERS who kept the pipe open. | HIGH (enforcement direction is clear even though the exact registration trigger for the strategy seller is fact-specific) |
| **AI research using the USER's OWN Claude/Gemini/OpenAI API key**, Xorlog only supplies UI/orchestration/prompts, model output is not authored by a human RA at Xorlog | Genuinely unsettled — no SEBI rule directly addresses BYOK AI | [INTERPRETATION] Two readings pull opposite ways. **(a) Tool reading:** if Xorlog's AI answers general research questions (financial-statement Q&A, "explain this ratio," neutral analysis) without emitting a specific buy/sell/hold verdict on a named security to many people, this sits inside the same "tool, not advice" bucket as a screener — the fact that inference runs on the user's own key strengthens the "it's the user's compute, not Xorlog's opinion" framing. **(b) Substance-over-form reading:** SEBI's stated enforcement posture is that disclaimers don't matter, only substance does [DATA lawyervikasgupta.com — "SEBI examines actual substance"], and SEBI's AI/ML consultation paper (Jun-2025) proposes that a regulated entity is "solely liable for AI/ML tools it uses, whether developed in-house or procured externally" regardless of whose model/key it is [DATA docs.getaxonflow.com summarizing Reg 16C language]. If Xorlog's product designs prompts that reliably produce per-security buy/sell/hold output, reaches many users, and is monetized (directly or via the paid-plan upsell), SEBI would likely look through the "it's the user's key" framing exactly as it looked through "education" framing in the finfluencer cases. **Practical implication: keep BYOK AI outputs framed as analysis/explanation, not verdicts, until this is cleared by counsel; do not let the paid-research-plan tier and the BYOK tier structurally converge on the same buy/sell/hold output.** | LOW/UNSETTLED — flag for lawyer explicitly, this is the single most novel question in the whole brief |
| **Publishing Xorlog's own paper-trading/model-portfolio track record publicly** — even without saying "buy now" | RA registration (as of the Dec-2024 amendment) | The Third Amendment Regs 2024 + Jan-2025 guidelines explicitly bring **"model portfolios" and "trading calls"** into RA-regulated "research services," with a full compliance framework: defined selection methodology, labelling, investment horizon, rebalancing rules communicated with rationale, and mandatory third-party-validated performance benchmarking [DATA streetgains.in, taxguru.in — SEBI Updates Regulations for Research Analysts 2025]. This closes the "it's just a track record, not advice" loophole the task asked about — SEBI now treats a published model/paper portfolio of named securities as research requiring registration, independent of whether a "buy now" CTA is attached. | HIGH — this is the clearest, most directly-on-point rule found in this research pass |
| **Paid research/recommendations plan** — specific buy/sell/hold calls, price targets, stock-specific opinions, delivered for a fee (directly or bundled) | RA registration mandatory | Core definition of "research report"/"research analyst" under the RA Regs; FAQ confirms "research bundled with services... is considered 'research for consideration' even if charged indirectly" [DATA taxguru.in FAQ analysis]. | HIGH |
| **Paid newsletter with specific stock calls** | RA registration mandatory (same as above) | Same "research for consideration" test applies regardless of format (newsletter vs report vs app feed) or subscription-length gimmick. Precedent: Equitymaster (long-running paid stock newsletter) operates as a registered RA and was the subject of a Sept-2025 SEBI informal-guidance letter specifically about its multi-year subscription/fee-cap practices [DATA maknassociates.com]. | HIGH |
| **"Educational" content / courses / webinars** — general concepts, no specific stock calls | None, IF genuinely general | Exemption covers "general views on the market or any industry/sector," training/workshops on research methodology [DATA cskruti.com]. **BUT** the Asmita Patel (Feb-2025) and Avadhut Sathe (Dec-2025) orders show SEBI will pierce an "education" label the moment the substance is stock/options tips, "urge to buy/sell," or return-linked promises delivered via Telegram/Zoom/courses for a fee — disclaimers do not help [DATA taxguru.in, businesstoday.in]. | HIGH for genuine education; HIGH RISK the moment any per-stock call or performance promise creeps in |
| **Charging a software/subscription fee purely for the backtesting tool itself** (not for any recommendation content) | None, for the pure-tool charge | RA/IA Regs regulate the SERVICE of giving recommendations/advice for consideration, not charging money for software. Precedent: AlgoTest, Streak (pre-2024 paid tiers), TradesViz, TradingView all charge SaaS fees without RA/IA licences because the fee buys compute/UX, not a recommendation. | MEDIUM-HIGH [INTERPRETATION] — if the "backtesting result" itself functions as a signal broadcast to many subscribers (e.g. "this pre-built strategy backtests at 40% CAGR, subscribe to auto-run it"), it tips back toward the strategy-marketplace row above |
| **Personalized advice** tailored to an individual's goals/risk/portfolio (robo-advisory style) | Investment Adviser (IA) registration, NOT RA | SEBI's 2016 consultation paper confirmed automated/algorithmic advice tools fall under IA Regs 2013 when they profile the client and tailor output to that profile — "provision of advice on securities...tailored to client circumstances falls under Investment Adviser regulations," with mandatory risk-profiling, suitability records (5-yr retention), and full IA compliance even for a "robo" front-end [DATA cskruti.com robo-advisor summary]. | HIGH |
| **API-based execution helper** — user's own broker API key, Xorlog UI sends ONE order per explicit user click, zero Xorlog-side logic deciding trade timing/sizing | Likely none (functions as an order-entry terminal), but boundary is fact-specific and the framework is new | [INTERPRETATION] The Feb-2025 algo circular's classification hinges on whether the ORDER LOGIC is algorithmic, not on whether a human clicks confirm. A pure "click here to place the exact order you specified" front-end is closer to a trading terminal than an "algo." The moment any strategy/scanner output auto-populates or auto-fires an order (even with a confirm click), the safer reading is that this is now "algo trading" requiring exchange empanelment. **This is the single highest-uncertainty item for Xorlog's roadmap and must be confirmed with the empanelling broker + a securities lawyer before building**, especially since one secondary source claims third-party servers/cloud infra are barred outright unless integrated with the broker's own system [UNVERIFIED — groww.in, stronger claim than other sources, needs primary-circular confirmation]. | LOW-MEDIUM — genuinely ambiguous, verify before building |
| **Algo/strategy-marketplace with auto-execution** (Xorlog hosts strategies that auto-place orders via a broker API) | Exchange empanelment as an "Algo Provider" + broker partnership mandatory; black-box strategies ALSO require RA registration of the algo-provider entity | Direct, explicit requirement under the Feb-2025 circular — see Algo Framework section below. | HIGH |

## RA Registration Playbook

### Steps, in order
1. **Choose entity form.** Individual/partnership (cheap, but a solo/small-team RA business) vs. Private Limited/LLP (needed if Xorlog wants to scale a branded, multi-analyst, app-embedded research arm — this is almost certainly the right form for Xorlog's eventual research entity).
2. **Principal Officer (and any client-facing staff) clear NISM-Series-XV** (Research Analyst Certification Exam). Client-facing staff ("Persons Associated with Research Services," PARS) need a graduate degree (any field) + the same NISM cert [DATA cskruti.com amendment summary].
3. **Enlist with BASL** (BSE Administration & Supervision Ltd, which SEBI has recognised as the Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body, RAASB) — **this enlistment is a prerequisite before the SEBI application can even be filed** [DATA taxguru.in BASL summary].
4. **Compile documents**: education/NISM certificates, KYC/identity, business proof, Principal Officer undertaking, deposit proof.
5. **Submit Form A** via the SEBI Intermediary Portal / BSE IA-RA portal with the registration fee.
6. **SEBI review** — typically several rounds of clarification (10–20 working days per round per secondary-source estimates); grant of a unique **INH-prefixed registration number** on approval.
7. **Ongoing**: maintain the security deposit (marked as lien to RAASB), file periodic compliance/AI-tool-usage disclosures, follow model-portfolio methodology/benchmarking rules if publishing any model portfolio.

### Qualification / education requirements
- Graduate degree in finance, accountancy, business management, commerce, economics, capital markets, banking, insurance, actuarial science, or "other financial services," from a government-recognised institution — OR a professional qualification (CA/CFA/MBA-Finance) — OR a 1-year NISM PG diploma in securities markets [DATA cskruti.com].
- No separate minimum-experience requirement is currently mandated for the base qualification path (older 5-yr-experience requirement appears in some secondary summaries but is contradicted by others describing the Dec-2024 relaxation — **[UNVERIFIED]**, reconcile with a lawyer before relying on it).
- A Nov-2025 SEBI amendment reportedly further relaxes this to "any graduate," pending formal notification as of mid-2026 [UNVERIFIED / pending — multiple sources flag this as "media reports suggest," not yet a notified rule as of the research date].
- NISM-Series-XV: mandatory for all RA applicants and PARS staff. ₹1,500 exam fee [DATA nismexams.in-style sources], revised syllabus effective 20-Jan-2026, 100 questions/100 marks, 60 to pass, 25% negative marking per wrong answer, certificate valid 3 years.

### Financial requirements
- **Net-worth certificate abolished** — replaced by a **security deposit** (cash/FD/liquid-MF units, marked as lien in favour of RAASB), tiered by number of clients [DATA multiple sources converge on this figure]:
  | Clients | Deposit |
  |---|---|
  | Up to 150 | ₹1 lakh |
  | 151–300 | ₹2 lakh |
  | 301–1,000 | ₹5 lakh |
  | 1,001+ | ₹10 lakh |
- **Registration fee** (figures as found, 2026): Individual/Partnership Firm **₹15,000 + GST**; Private Limited Company/LLP **₹5,50,000 + GST** [DATA appeared independently in two separate searches — PrideCons guide and general-search synthesis]. RA registration is now **perpetual/lifetime** (the old 5-year renewal cycle has been abolished, subject to continued compliance) [DATA taxguru.in BASL summary]. **[UNVERIFIED note]**: one secondary source (taxguru comprehensive-FAQ analysis) instead cited "₹3,000 for 5 years, ₹1,000 renewal" — this looks like stale pre-amendment data given the independently-corroborated ₹15,000/₹5.5L figures and the "now perpetual" language found elsewhere; treat the ₹15,000/₹5,50,000+GST figures as more likely current but verify directly on the SEBI/BASL portal before budgeting.
- **BASL/RAASB membership fee**: for the comparable Investment Adviser track, BASL charges ₹6,000 new / ₹5,400 renewal (individual/partnership) or ₹3,00,000 new / ₹2,97,000 renewal (body corporate/LLP), billed for a 3-year block [DATA taxguru.in BASL FAQ]. The RA-specific RAASB administrative fee is described only as "as specified by RAASB/IAASB" in sources found — **exact current RA-track BASL fee not pinned down in this pass; confirm directly with BASL/RAASB before budgeting.**
- **Client fee cap**: ₹1,51,000 per family per annum for individual/HUF (non-accredited) clients, excluding statutory charges, reviewed every 3 years via Cost Inflation Index [DATA taxguru.in, confirmed via Equitymaster informal-guidance letter Sept-2025]. AUA-mode fee (for IAs, comparable concept) capped at 2.5%/annum.
- **Advance fee**: originally capped at 1 quarter by the Dec-2024 amendment, then **relaxed in April-2025 to a 1-year cap**, with mandatory pro-rata refund and no breakage fee on early termination [DATA multiple sources, cross-confirmed by the Equitymaster informal guidance].

### Realistic end-to-end timeline
**4–8 months**, driven mostly by NISM exam-slot availability, not SEBI processing speed: ~1–2 months NISM-XV prep, up to ~3 months waiting for an exam slot in metro cities (worse in Tier-2/3), then ~2–3 months for BASL enlistment + Form-A filing + SEBI review/clarification rounds (SEBI's own review window is cited at 60–90 days once documents are complete) [DATA lawyervikasgupta.com, profsheetalkunderacademy.com].

### Part-time RA option
SEBI now formally recognises **Part-Time Research Analysts** — a person with another primary job can register as RA with a max of 75 clients (employer NOC required, must label communications "Part-Time Research Analyst"); beyond 75 clients, must convert to full-time [DATA cskruti.com, taxguru.in]. Not directly useful for Xorlog as a company product, but relevant if any individual team member wants to be the named RA before the company itself is registered.

### RA vs Investment Adviser (IA) — why RA is the right first licence
| | Research Analyst (RA) | Investment Adviser (IA) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature of service | One-to-many: general research, recommendations, model portfolios, price targets — client acts at their own discretion | One-to-one: personalised advice tailored to a specific client's goals/income/risk profile |
| Fiduciary duty | No | Yes — legally bound to act in client's best interest |
| Can earn distribution/commission income | Individual RAs cannot distribute mutual funds; conflict-of-interest rules apply for non-individuals | Cannot earn commissions from selling products (must be fee-only) — even more restrictive |
| Certification | NISM-Series-XV | NISM-Series-X-A and X-B |
| Registration fee (2026, as found) | ₹15,000+GST individual / ₹5,50,000+GST company | ~₹5,000 application + ₹10,000 on approval (individual) [DATA lawyervikasgupta.com] — cheaper on paper, but... |
| Ongoing burden | Model-portfolio methodology/benchmark disclosures, fee caps | Mandatory risk-profiling + signed advisory agreement + suitability records for every client, 5-yr retention — much heavier per-client compliance load |
| Fit for a "recommendations feed" product | **Yes — this is the natural licence for a one-to-many paid research/recommendations plan**, which is exactly what Xorlog's future paid plan is | Wrong shape — IA is for bespoke financial-planning-style advice, not a subscription content feed |
| Dual registration | SEBI now permits an entity to hold BOTH RA and IA registration subject to conflict-of-interest conditions (cannot serve the same client under both hats simultaneously) [DATA search synthesis] | — |

**Conclusion for Xorlog: the paid research/recommendations plan should be built as an RA-registered offering (own subsidiary, once ready, or third-party-RA-sourced content in the interim). IA registration should only be pursued later, and only if Xorlog pivots toward genuinely personalised, risk-profiled advisory — a heavier, different business, not the natural next step from a screener/journal/recs product.**

## Algo Framework Obligations

**Governing circular**: SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013, "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading," dated **4-Feb-2025** [DATA sebi.gov.in].

### Timeline (as reconstructed from multiple sources — some conflict on exact dates, noted)
- **4-Feb-2025**: circular issued.
- **1-Apr-2025**: Brokers' Industry Standards Forum (ISF) required to finalise detailed implementation standards.
- **1-Aug-2025**: original industry compliance deadline.
- **1-Oct-2025**: deadline extended (after industry feedback) for full framework implementation.
- **1-Apr-2026**: multiple 2026 sources describe this as the date the framework becomes **fully mandatory** for all stock brokers, i.e. the "hard" enforcement date most commonly cited in current (mid-2026) commentary [DATA multiple 2026 blog sources — cskruti.com, tradejini.com, algobulls.com]. **[UNVERIFIED]** — exact sequencing across the Aug-2025/Oct-2025/Apr-2026 dates differs slightly by source; treat Apr-2026 as the operative "must be compliant by now" date given today's date (16-Jul-2026), and confirm the precise clause-by-clause effective dates against the primary circular before relying on any specific date for a compliance decision.

### Core obligations
- **Brokers**: solely responsible for every algo running through their platform; must implement systems to detect/tag orders exceeding the order-per-second (OPS) threshold as algo orders; assign a **unique vendor-client-specific API key**; **whitelist static IPs**; enforce **OAuth-only login + mandatory two-factor authentication** for every API session; may deal ONLY with exchange-**empanelled** Algo Providers; must field complaints related to their empanelled Algo Providers; subject to exchange inspection.
- **Exchanges**: assign a unique **Algo-ID** to every algorithmic strategy (in-house or third-party) before live deployment — "plug-and-play" unvetted strategies are no longer permitted; specify empanelment criteria/procedure for Algo Providers (still being notified/operationalised in stages as of this research pass).
- **Algo Providers** (third-party SaaS platforms or independent developers offering order-placing facilities via API): must be **empanelled with the stock exchanges**; act legally as **agents of the broker** and are subject to the broker's due diligence; **cannot connect directly to exchanges** — must route through a registered, empanelled broker's system. One secondary source states third-party servers/cloud infrastructure are barred outright "unless integrated with the broker system" [UNVERIFIED — groww.in, stronger than other sources; verify against the primary circular].
- **White-box vs black-box classification**:
  - *White-box (execution algos)*: logic fully transparent/disclosed to the investor — gets a faster registration/empanelment track.
  - *Black-box (non-disclosed algos)*: logic proprietary/opaque to the user — the provider **must additionally register as a SEBI Research Analyst**, publish and maintain a research report per algo (methodology, performance, risk metrics), and obtain fresh registration on any change to the underlying logic [DATA cskruti.com, tradetron.tech, multiple 2026 blogs converge on this].
- **Order-per-second (OPS) threshold**: most commonly cited figure is **10 orders/second per exchange per calendar second** as the line below which a retail investor's self-built algo does NOT require empanelment [DATA tradejini.com practical-guide summary] — **[UNVERIFIED exact number]**, one primary-adjacent legal source described the threshold as merely "to be notified in due course" as of its writing; treat 10 OPS as the best current estimate, confirm against the live exchange circular before architecting Xorlog's execution-helper rate limits.
- **Self-built retail algos exemption**: an individual who builds their own algo strictly for personal use (not offered/sold to others), used only across themself + immediate family (spouse, dependent children/parents), and staying under the OPS threshold, is exempt from empanelment [DATA cskruti.com].

### What this means for Xorlog's "API-based execution helper" specifically
The circular's classification test turns on whether the **order logic is algorithmic**, not on whether a human clicks "confirm." [INTERPRETATION] A feature where Xorlog's UI simply transmits the exact order parameters a user manually typed/selected, one order per explicit click, with zero Xorlog-side decision logic, sits closest to a trading-terminal/OMS front-end and is the safest place to start. The moment any Xorlog-side scanner/strategy output auto-populates an order for one-click submission — let alone auto-fires without a click — the far more conservative and defensible reading is that this is now "algo trading" requiring: (a) a partnership with a registered broker who empanels Xorlog as its Algo Provider, (b) exchange approval/Algo-ID for the specific strategy logic, and (c) if the logic is not fully disclosed to the user, RA registration for the Xorlog entity providing it. **This is a hard gate — do not build auto-populate-and-fire execution features before this is confirmed with the empanelling broker and a securities lawyer.**

### Enforcement precedent directly on point
March-2026 SEBI adjudication orders penalised **10 stock brokers** (RK Stockholding, Samco Securities, Shri Parasram Holdings, Ganganagar Commodity, Northeast Broking Services, ITI Securities Broking, Ashlar Securities, ATS Share Brokers, First Global Stockbroking, Pentad Securities) a total of **₹16 lakh** (ATS Share Brokers individually ₹2 lakh) for continuing to deal/integrate with algo platform **Tradetron** after a Sept-2022 circular required brokers to disassociate from any platform referencing hosted strategies' past/expected "assured" or "consistent" returns — several brokers only disconnected in mid-2023, well past the 7-day compliance window given [DATA moneylife.in, sebi.gov.in enforcement orders mar-2026]. **Lesson for Xorlog: it is the BROKER PARTNER, not only the algo/strategy platform, that SEBI penalises for enabling non-compliant return-claim marketing — any future broker-partnership deal Xorlog signs will make the broker directly exposed to whatever performance claims live on Xorlog's platform.**

## How Incumbents Are Structured

| Product | RA/IA registered? | Entity / reg. no. | Structural pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Screener.in** | Not found registered | — | Pure quantitative/fundamental-data screener; user writes their own query; no first-party buy/sell/hold label attached to any stock — relies on the "presenting financial data...in an easy to understand manner" exemption. |
| **Chartink** | Not found registered | — | Same pattern for technical scans — user-built scan syntax, no house recommendation attributed to Chartink itself. **[MEDIUM confidence — no explicit public statement found either way, inferred from absence of any RA registration reference.]** |
| **Tickertape** | Explicitly NOT an RA/IA | Anchorage Technologies Pvt Ltd (ATPL); separately AMFI mutual-fund-distributor ARN-253554 | Platform disclaims itself as "only a technology provider... not an intermediary" and states content "must not be construed to be investment advice or research analysis" [DATA tickertape.in/meta/disclosures]. Any actual recommendation content (smallcases, analyst-consensus aggregation) is sourced from separately-licensed third parties. |
| **Trendlyne** | **RA-registered** | Windmill Capital Private Limited, SEBI Reg. **INH200007645** | Unlike Tickertape/Screener, Trendlyne publishes its own first-party "Durability/Valuation" scores and checklist-style calls/StratQ strategies — registered because it is itself issuing security-specific opinions, not just neutral data. |
| **Sensibull** | **RA-registered** | Riskilla Software Technologies Pvt Ltd, SEBI Reg. **INH200006895** | Registered because its options-strategy/signal features (research.sensibull.com) surface first-party trade ideas, not just a neutral option-chain calculator. |
| **Univest** | **RA-registered** | Uniresearch Global Private Limited (Univest subsidiary), SEBI Reg. **INH000013776**, registered 30-Nov-2023 | Broker + RA hybrid — bundles ₹5/trade brokerage with RA-licensed advisory tiers. This is explicitly NOT the non-broker model Xorlog wants, but shows the "subsidiary carries the RA licence" pattern. |
| **smallcase** | Not registered itself | — | Pure infrastructure layer: "does not provide any research recommendations on its own"; every smallcase is authored and owned by a separately SEBI-registered RA/IA/PMS "manager." |
| **Streak / Tradetron** | Not registered as RA/IA themselves | — | Self-described "neutral technology service providers"; the platform disclaims responsibility for the STRATEGY content, pushing any RA/IA registration need onto the strategy creator/seller. **Limit on this defense**: see the ATS Share Brokers enforcement case above — SEBI went after the brokers dealing with Tradetron once return-claims proliferated on the platform, regardless of the platform's own disclaimer. |
| **AlgoTest** | Not registered itself | — | Cleanly splits "the recommendation" from "the automation": offers "RA Algos" = strategies built BY independently SEBI-registered Research Analysts, which AlgoTest then automates/executes on its rails. AlgoTest's own backtesting/execution layer stays unregistered. |
| **Quantsapp** | **RA-registered** (advisory arm) | Shubham Agarwal / "Quantsapp Advisory," SEBI Reg. **INH000006332** | The signal-generation/advisory layer is separately RA-licensed under the named proprietor; the broader options-analytics app wraps around it. |

### Pattern synthesis (directly actionable for Xorlog)
Nearly every incumbent uses the same **split structure**: (1) an unregistered pure-technology/infrastructure layer (screener, charting, execution rails, journaling, backtesting) that stays outside RA/IA scope by strictly never issuing a first-party security-specific buy/sell/hold call, plus (2) a separately-branded, SEBI-registered legal entity (sometimes a subsidiary — Univest/Uniresearch, Trendlyne/Windmill Capital, Sensibull/Riskilla — sometimes a wholly independent third party publishing on top of the platform — smallcase managers, AlgoTest's "RA Algos") that alone carries responsibility for the actual recommendation/signal/model-portfolio content. **This is the direct template for Xorlog's roadmap**: launch the tool layer (journal, screener, backtester, BYOK AI research scoped to analysis-not-verdicts) unregistered, and when the paid recommendations plan is ready, either (a) stand up a separate RA-registered subsidiary, or (b) host already-RA-registered third-party analysts' content first (lower time-to-revenue, matches the smallcase/AlgoTest model).

## Recommended Sequencing

**Phase 0 — Day 1, no licence needed (build now):**
- Trade journaling (private log, no output shared/sold to others).
- Screener kept strictly at the "neutral data / sector-index technical" level — no first-party buy/sell/hold label on any named security.
- Backtesting/strategy-builder tool — manual configuration, no auto-execution, and critically: **no "assured"/"consistent"/guaranteed-return language anywhere on any strategy result**, per the direct Tradetron-brokers enforcement precedent.
- Educational content — general concepts, never a per-stock call, never urging liquidation/borrowing to pay for anything (the exact pattern that sank Asmita Patel and Avadhut Sathe).
- BYO-key AI research — scope prompts/output to analysis and explanation ("what does this ratio mean," "summarize this filing"), not templated buy/sell/hold verdicts on named securities, until the BYOK/RA boundary question above is cleared by counsel.
- Publishing Xorlog's own backtested-strategy performance as methodology-and-cost-transparent research **about a strategy**, not as a named-basket "model portfolio" with an implied ongoing recommendation (the latter is squarely RA-regulated per the Dec-2024 model-portfolio rule).

**Phase 1 — Day 1 but with a hard architectural guardrail:**
- API-based execution helper limited to: user manually specifies the order, clicks confirm, Xorlog fires exactly that one order via the user's own broker API key, with **zero Xorlog-side logic** deciding what/when/how much to trade. Treat any auto-populate-from-strategy or auto-fire feature as out of scope until Phase 3's compliance work is done.

**Phase 2 — requires RA registration; start the ~4–8 month process in parallel with Phase 0/1 build, not after:**
- Paid research/recommendations plan (specific buy/sell/hold, price targets).
- Any first-party model portfolio / paper track record marketed as actionable.
- Paid newsletters containing specific stock calls.
- Recommended structure: incorporate a separate Pvt Ltd RA entity (₹5,50,000+GST fee tier, tiered deposit ₹1–10 lakh, BASL enlistment) mirroring the Trendlyne/Sensibull/Univest split-entity pattern — **or**, to get a revenue-generating recs feature live faster, initially host already-RA-registered third-party analysts' content (the smallcase/AlgoTest "RA Algos" model) while Xorlog's own RA licence is in process.

**Phase 3 — requires a broker partnership + exchange empanelment, only once the Apr-2026 algo framework is confirmed stable and its exact obligations verified against the primary circular:**
- Any strategy-marketplace, auto-execution, or signal-to-order automation feature: must partner with an empanelled broker, obtain an exchange Algo-ID per strategy, and — if the logic is not fully disclosed to the user (black-box) — register the algo-provider entity as an RA in addition.
- Never market "assured"/"consistent" returns on any hosted strategy — this is a live, directly-precedented enforcement trigger (ATS Share Brokers et al., Mar-2026) that exposes any future broker partner, not just Xorlog.

**Phase 4 — lowest priority, only with explicit Principal + compliance sign-off:**
- Personalized/robo-advisory (risk-profiled, individually-tailored advice) — requires a separate Investment Adviser (IA) registration, a heavier fiduciary/no-commission compliance regime that sits awkwardly next to an RA-registered recommendations business for the same client. Only pursue if the business model pivots toward genuine personalised advisory — it is not a natural next step from the tools/screener/recs stack.

**Cross-cutting rule for every phase**: disclaimers do not substitute for compliance. Every enforcement case surveyed (Asmita Patel, Avadhut Sathe, Yash Garg) involved a disclaimed "education"/"not advice" wrapper that SEBI disregarded because the underlying conduct — frequency, fee, specific security calls, performance promises — matched a regulated recommendation service. Xorlog's legal safety comes from what the product actually does, not from what its terms page says it does.

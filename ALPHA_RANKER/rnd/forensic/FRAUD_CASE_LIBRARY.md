## Epistemic status legend (D-035 discipline — this involves real companies/people)
- **ADJUDICATED/CONVICTED** — a court/tribunal has ruled on guilt, or SEBI's finding is final and unappealed/upheld.
- **REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING** — SEBI/SFIO/ED/CBI has taken formal action (order, chargesheet, arrest) but criminal/final adjudication is not complete, or is under appeal.
- **ALLEGATIONS-DISPUTED-NO-ADJUDICATION** — publicly reported allegations, company/promoter denies, no regulator/court finding of guilt.
- All rupee figures below are **[DATA]** only where they come from a specific cited order/judgment I can state with confidence; figures I recall as **widely-reported approximations** are marked **[INFERENCE]** with "~"; anything I cannot verify is marked **UNKNOWN**. This document was built from trained knowledge of extensively publicly documented cases (not live web verification — WebSearch/WebFetch were not invoked for this pass); before this library is used to make any real-money or reputational claim, a source-verification pass (exact order numbers/dates) is recommended and flagged as a gap below.
- No claim of guilt is made for any DISPUTED or CHARGES-PENDING case beyond what is cited.

## 1. Satyam Computer Services (2009)
**Status: ADJUDICATED/CONVICTED** — Ramalinga Raju's confession letter (7-Jan-2009) is public record; CBI special court convicted Raju + others in 2015 (later some sentences/fines revised on appeal); SEBI (2018 order, litigated) held auditors (PwC network partners) liable and barred them for a period. [DATA, widely reported/court record]

- **MO:** Fictitious sales invoices inflating revenue; forged bank statements/fixed-deposit receipts to show ~cash that did not exist; understated liabilities; fabricated interest income on non-existent deposits, sustained for years. [DATA]
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (A) margins/growth implausibly smooth vs IT-services peers over many quarters (too-good-to-be-true consistency); (B) reported cash & investments inconsistent with actual interest income yield (cash-and-investments-reality flag, §B); (C) promoter attempted a related-party acquisition of Maytas Properties/Maytas Infra using Satyam's own cash reserves (Dec-2008) — shareholder revolt over this deal is what triggered the collapse days later — classic related-party/self-dealing tell; (C) very long single-auditor tenure with no rotation; whistle-blower allegations years before (reported, e.g. anonymous emails to independent directors) not acted on.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** Margin-smoothness / cash-vs-interest-income mismatch is **data-screenable** (accruals, cash-yield check). The Maytas related-party acquisition attempt and whistle-blower history are **filing/news-read-only** — no structured field captures "promoter tried to buy a related company with our cash."
- **One-line lesson:** the single biggest tell was the self-dealing acquisition attempt, not the accounting ratios — governance red flags can fire before earnings-quality ratios do.

## 2. Gitanjali Gems / Mehul Choksi — PNB LoU fraud (2018)
**Status: REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING** — Choksi declared a fugitive economic offender; extradition litigation across jurisdictions (Belgium/Antigua) ongoing as of my knowledge; Nirav Modi (related but separate group) extradition from UK upheld; Indian criminal trial pending. [DATA: PNB's own Feb-2018 stock-exchange disclosure of the fraud is public record; extradition status per widely reported news, mark UNKNOWN for final case outcome.]

- **MO:** Fraudulent Letters of Undertaking (LoUs) issued by a single PNB branch (Brady House) without matching collateral or core-banking-system entry, used to draw unsecured "buyer's credit" from overseas branches of other Indian banks; funds allegedly round-tripped through related entities; combined exposure widely reported **~Rs 11,000–14,000 crore** [INFERENCE, approximate, contested figure across reports]. A SWIFT-vs-core-banking reconciliation gap at the bank (not the borrower) is the structural enabler.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (D) rapid unsecured guarantee-based credit growth at the borrower with no matching collateral registered; (C) multiple related-entity conduits in the gems & jewellery trade (classic opacity sector); (B) receivables/inventory levels implausible for the reported turnover in a sector where physical-stock verification is hard; (C) promoter share pledging.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** The core enabling control failure (SWIFT-CBS gap) sits **inside the bank**, not the borrower's filings — **not screenable from equity-side company data at all**; it is a banking-system audit finding. What IS screenable on the borrower side: receivables/inventory-vs-sales anomalies (§A) and promoter pledge trend (§C) — both **data-screenable** in principle, IF pledge/inventory-turns data exists (see synthesis §Data gap below — ALPHA_RANKER currently has **no pledge column at all**, see `src/forensic/universe_forensic.py` line ~651).
- **One-line lesson:** some fraud enablers live in counterparty/bank control failures that no amount of borrower-side ratio analysis would ever surface — sector-level "known opacity" (gems/jewellery, LoU-heavy trade finance) is itself a prior that should raise baseline scrutiny.

## 3. Yes Bank / Rana Kapoor (2020)
**Status: MIXED — REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING (fraud/money-laundering allegations) + ADJUDICATED (RBI reconstruction/moratorium is a regulatory fact, not a fraud verdict)** — Kapoor arrested by ED (2020), chargesheeted in the DHFL-linked quid-pro-quo case; criminal trial ongoing as of my knowledge. RBI's moratorium and reconstruction scheme (March 2020) are settled regulatory facts. AT-1 bond write-off (~Rs 8,415 cr) [INFERENCE, widely reported] was separately litigated (Bombay HC ruled against the write-off in 2021; matter escalated) — mark that thread UNKNOWN/ongoing. [DATA for RBI action; REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING for the fraud allegations against Kapoor.]

- **MO:** Alleged evergreening of loans to stressed corporate groups; alleged kickbacks to Kapoor-family-linked entities (e.g., Doit Urban Ventures, Morgan Credits — named in ED chargesheets, allegations) in exchange for sanctioning/rolling over loans to specific stressed borrowers (DHFL among them); understated NPAs vs RBI's own divergence assessment; rapid AT-1 issuance used to shore up capital.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (D) large gap between bank-reported NPAs and RBI's supervisory "divergence" once disclosed (this IS a published, structured data point in annual reports post-2018 RBI mandate); (B) loan-book growth concentrated in a handful of stressed group names (ADAG, DHFL, IL&FS, Essel) — concentration-to-known-stress-names is visible from disclosed large-exposure data; (C) promoter/CEO-linked private entities receiving payments contemporaneous with loan sanctions to borrowers (kickback pattern) — this is filing-invisible, only visible via ED's independent financial-flow tracing.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** NPA-divergence disclosure and loan-concentration-to-stressed-names are **data-screenable** (if large-exposure/sectoral concentration data is captured — ALPHA_RANKER's fundamentals pipeline does not currently ingest RBI divergence reports or large-exposure disclosures — **gap**). The kickback/related-entity cash-flow tracing is **filing/investigation-read-only**, effectively invisible to any market-data screen.
- **One-line lesson:** a bank's own disclosed NPA-divergence number (RBI-mandated since 2018) is a rare case of a *structured, comparable* red flag hiding in plain sight in annual reports — worth a dedicated field if covering financials.

## 4. DHFL — Dewan Housing Finance (Wadhawan brothers) (2019–2020)
**Status: REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING** — CBI/ED chargesheeted the Wadhawans in 2022 in what was then called India's largest bank-fraud case (~Rs 34,000 crore, reported) [INFERENCE, widely reported figure]; NCLT-driven resolution (acquired by Piramal Capital, 2021) is a settled fact; criminal trial outcome UNKNOWN as of my knowledge.

- **MO:** Loans extended to a large number (widely reported as ~80) of shell/shell-like entities with negligible real operations, sharing addresses/directors with promoter-linked entities; a parallel/unrecorded loan book alleged by a 2019 investigative exposé ("Bandra Book", ~Rs 14,000 cr alleged, [INFERENCE — allegation, not fully adjudicated]) not reflected in statutory filings; proceeds allegedly used to acquire personal/promoter-linked assets.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (B) loan-book/AUM growth far outpacing disbursement quality and sector peers; (C) large "project loans" to unrated, unlisted entities registered at addresses matching promoter-group entities (related-party-by-address, a specific filing-read tell — company-master/MCA address matching); (A) securitization gains booked upfront inflating reported profit quality; (D) rating agencies held ratings high until very close to default (ratings-lag flag).
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** AUM-growth-vs-peer and rating-trajectory are **data-screenable** (growth-rate anomaly + rating history, if ingested — ALPHA_RANKER's `src/forensic/universe_forensic.py` computes debt/EBITDA and interest-cover trends but does not currently ingest credit-rating-agency action history — **gap**). Address-matching between borrower entities and promoter-group entities requires **MCA/ROC filing reads**, not screenable from screener-style fundamentals.
- **One-line lesson:** shell-company lending leaves a footprint in *related-party address overlap*, which is a filing-read task, not a ratio — the ratio evidence (AUM growth, securitization-gain reliance) is only corroborating, not sufficient alone.

## 5. IL&FS — Infrastructure Leasing & Financial Services (2018)
**Status: REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING** — Government superseded the board via NCLT (Oct-2018); SFIO chargesheets filed against several former promoters/executives (e.g., Ravi Parthasarathy and others); SEBI has passed some orders against former executives/rating analysts; final criminal adjudication outcomes UNKNOWN as of my knowledge.

- **MO:** A group of 300+ subsidiaries/SPVs used to roll over short-term debt and obscure group-level stress; related-party inter-company lending (ICDs) between group entities; asset-liability duration mismatch (short-term commercial paper funding long-gestation infrastructure assets); credit ratings held at AAA until days before the first default.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (C) opaque, unusually large multi-entity group structure with heavy inter-company lending — this is the single clearest, most repeatable §C tell across this entire library; (B) short-term-borrowing-funding-long-gestation-assets is a classic asset-liability mismatch visible from balance-sheet tenor breakdowns, IF disclosed; (D) sudden multi-notch rating downgrade from AAA to D within months — a *rating-cliff* is itself a lagging but structured signal; independent directors' resignations/whistle-blowing shortly before collapse.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** Group-structure complexity (subsidiary count, ICD volume) and rating trajectory are **partially data-screenable** if group-structure/ICD disclosures are ingested (ALPHA_RANKER does not currently ingest inter-company-loan schedules across a group — **gap**, this sits in AR notes/related-party schedules, i.e. **filing-read-only** in practice today). The rating-cliff itself, once it starts, is data-screenable but by definition arrives **after** the ex-ante window has mostly closed.
- **One-line lesson:** AAA-to-D in months is proof that credit ratings are a lagging, not leading, indicator for group-structure fraud — the group-complexity/ICD-web itself is the true leading indicator, and it lives in AR footnotes, not the standard fundamentals schema.

## 6. Manpasand Beverages (2018)
**Status: REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING** — Deloitte (auditor) resigned May-2018 citing serious concerns over fake sales/GST-invoice irregularities (Deloitte's resignation letter is public record filed with exchanges); SFIO/EOW chargesheeted promoter Dhirendra Singh and others for GST/invoice fraud; trial outcome UNKNOWN as of my knowledge; company later delisted/into insolvency.

- **MO:** Fake sales routed through fictitious/related distributor entities to inflate revenue and to fraudulently claim GST input-tax-credit; channel stuffing; receivables against buyers with no real underlying trade.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (C) **auditor resignation mid-year citing fraud concerns — this is 08_FORENSICS_REDFLAGS.md's own §Hard-veto-list item #1**, the cleanest, least ambiguous ex-ante signal in this entire library; (A) receivable-days ballooning far beyond FMCG-peer norms; (A) revenue growth disconnected from independently-verifiable proxies (GST e-way-bill/GST-filing data, where accessible, vs reported sales) — a genuinely external cross-check, not self-reported.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** Auditor resignation is **directly data-screenable** if auditor-change events are ingested as a structured field (ALPHA_RANKER's 08 doc lists auditor resignation as hard-veto but `src/forensic/universe_forensic.py`'s current flag set does NOT show an auditor-change/resignation field being computed — **confirmed gap, highest-priority fix**). Receivable-days-vs-peer is **data-screenable** today (receivables/sales growth flags exist, sparse coverage noted in the code). GST cross-checks are **external-data/filing-read-only** (not in this repo's data catalog).
- **One-line lesson:** auditor resignation-for-cause is the cheapest, hardest, least-noisy hard-veto signal in the whole taxonomy — and it is currently a documented gap, not a live flag, in ALPHA_RANKER's pipeline.

## 9. Cox & Kings (2019)
**Status: REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING** — SFIO/ED investigation into the Kerkar family (Peter Kerkar and others) alleging fund diversion via the group's forex/prepaid-card financial-services arm; chargesheets reported filed; trial outcome UNKNOWN as of my knowledge. Company defaulted on commercial paper/NCDs in Sept-2019 despite reporting healthy cash balances just before — this default itself is a settled market fact.

- **MO (as alleged):** Siphoning through related-party financial-services subsidiary (forex/travel-card business) via circular transactions disguised as inter-company loans/business transactions; unsecured commercial-paper issuance funding an unrelated financial subsidiary; default despite reported strong cash & investments.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (B) **"large reported cash + high debt/sudden default" is literally §B's own named red flag** ("cash & investments reality... large cash + high debt = suspicious") — this case is closest to a textbook match for an existing taxonomy line; (C) related-party loans/advances ballooning as a share of the balance sheet; (B) rapid unsecured CP issuance funding a financial-services subsidiary outside the core travel business (business-model drift funding opacity).
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** Cash-vs-debt-vs-default and related-party-loan-share are **data-screenable today** if cash/investments and related-party-loan line items are populated (contingent-liabilities and net-debt/EBITDA flags exist in `universe_forensic.py`; related-party-loan-as-%-of-balance-sheet is NOT currently a computed flag — **gap**, it needs AR-note-level related-party schedule, i.e. partially **filing-read**).
- **One-line lesson:** reported cash balance is not verified cash — a company that defaults weeks after reporting a large cash pile has almost always had that cash tied up in related-party instruments; §B's existing "cash reality" flag is under-weighted relative to how often it recurs.

## 10. Zee Entertainment / Essel Group (Subhash Chandra) (2019, ongoing)
**Status: ALLEGATIONS-DISPUTED / REGULATORY-ACTION-PENDING** — SEBI's 2022 interim order alleged fund diversion (~Rs 200 crore, [INFERENCE, widely reported figure]) by Subhash Chandra/Punit Goenka via related shell entities; this order was stayed/modified on appeal (SAT); Goenka was later reinstated as MD after interim relief. No final adjudication of guilt. Separately, the Jan-2019 mutual-fund invocation of promoter share pledges is a **settled market event**, not itself an adjudicated fraud — it was the *consequence* of Essel group's inter-corporate-deposit (ICD) stress, disclosed by the promoters themselves in a public letter to MF trustees (Jan-2019).

- **MO:** Essel Group's promoter entities had borrowed heavily via inter-corporate deposits from mutual funds, pledging Zee shares as collateral, to fund group infrastructure/EPC businesses under stress; when Zee's share price fell, lenders moved to invoke pledges, creating a listed-company contagion event from an unlisted-group-entity problem. Separately (allegations), SEBI alleged Zee-entity funds were diverted to shell companies for personal use.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (C) **rising promoter pledge percentage — one of the cleanest, most repeatedly-recurring §C flags in this entire library** (also PC Jeweller, also Educomp-near-100%); (C) complex multi-entity group structure (media co + EPC/infra co + financial arms) obscuring where group cash actually sits; (B) group-level ICD web outside the listed entity's own balance sheet, invisible to a single-company forensic screen.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** Promoter pledge trend is **data-screenable in principle and one of the most bang-for-buck flags to fix** — but ALPHA_RANKER currently has **zero pledge data** (`src/forensic/universe_forensic.py`: "no column in any dataset checked... Explicit insufficient-data hook"). This is the single highest-value, cheapest fix identified across all 15 cases. Group-level ICD exposure (money leaving the listed entity to fund unlisted group companies) is **filing-read-only** (related-party-loan AR notes) unless a group-mapping dataset is built.
- **One-line lesson:** promoter pledge is a top-tier recurring flag that ALPHA_RANKER currently cannot compute at all — this is the single most actionable data gap this library surfaces.

## 11. Ricoh India (2016)
**Status: ADJUDICATED (SEBI, partially, appeals continued)** — SEBI's 2020 order found the erstwhile management complicit in accounting fraud (fictitious lease-based revenue); the FY2015-16 accounts were formally restated turning a reported profit into a loss — this restatement is a settled, filed fact. Verifying the *existence* of the underlying leased assets requires an actual asset register/site check — **filing/physical-verification-only**, cannot be screened from financial statements alone.
- **One-line lesson:** a sudden shift in *what kind* of revenue a company books (not just how much) — e.g. hardware company suddenly booking large financing/leasing income — is itself a screenable business-model-mix anomaly, distinct from the standard growth/margin checks.

## 12. Amtek Auto (2015–2016)
**Status: ALLEGATIONS-DISPUTED / DEFAULT (not a major adjudicated fraud conviction to my knowledge)** — this is primarily a leverage-and-governance collapse (JM Financial MF gated redemptions in 2015 over Amtek Auto exposure) rather than a criminally adjudicated fraud; company went through IBC resolution (contested, protracted NCLT proceedings, eventual change of control). - **MO (as alleged/inferred from the leverage pattern):** Aggressive debt-funded global M&A (the promoter group's international auto-component acquisitions); related-party corporate guarantees and inter-group loans between Amtek Auto and its own subsidiaries/promoter-linked entities, obscuring true consolidated leverage; credit ratings held high until a sudden multi-notch downgrade.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (B) debt/EBITDA rising sharply through a string of acquisitions (classic §E "serial acquirer, rising leverage" pattern, though framed here as organic + M&A debt rather than pure related-party siphoning); (C) corporate guarantees extended to group entities without proportionate disclosure prominence; (D) rating-agency cliff-edge downgrade (AA-range to D-range within a short window) — the same ratings-lag pattern as IL&FS and DHFL.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** Debt/EBITDA trend and rating trajectory are **data-screenable** (debt/EBITDA flag exists in `universe_forensic.py`; rating-history ingestion is a **confirmed gap**, same as DHFL/IL&FS above — this is now the **third** case pointing at the same missing dataset). Corporate-guarantee disclosure quantum requires **AR contingent-liability-note reading** (contingent-liabilities flag exists in-code but is a coarse proxy, not guarantee-specific).
- **One-line lesson:** credit-rating-history ingestion is missing from ALPHA_RANKER and has now recurred as a gap across three independent cases (IL&FS, DHFL, Amtek) — this is a pattern, not a coincidence, and should be prioritized.

## 13. Kingfisher Airlines / Vijay Mallya (2012 collapse; ongoing)

## 14. Educomp Solutions (2013 onward)
**Status: ALLEGATIONS-DISPUTED / DEFAULT (unadjudicated for fraud specifically)** — company defaulted, went through insolvency; auditors qualified accounts; no marquee criminal fraud conviction of promoters known to me as final/settled. Governance and accounting-aggressiveness issues are well documented via auditor qualifications and the company's own restated disclosures; label the "fraud" framing here as **allegations/aggressive-accounting**, not adjudicated fraud.

- **MO (as alleged/observed accounting practice):** Aggressive upfront revenue recognition on multi-year annuity-style "Smart Class" contracts — recognizing the full contract value at signing rather than over the service period — inflating reported revenue and profit well ahead of cash collection; receivables ballooned as collections lagged the front-loaded revenue; near-100% promoter pledge as the group tried to fund mounting losses; serial equity dilution (QIPs) to plug cash shortfalls.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (A) revenue-recognition policy visibly more aggressive than sector peers (upfront vs percentage-of-completion/over-the-contract-term) — detectable directly from the accounting-policy footnote, a specific filing-read item; (A) receivables growing far faster than cash collections/CFO (the same CFO/PAT-divergence pattern as PC Jeweller/Cox & Kings); (C) promoter pledge approaching ~100% — the most extreme pledge level in this entire library; (C) serial dilution/QIPs as a going-concern patch.
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** CFO/PAT divergence and receivables-vs-sales are **data-screenable today**. Extreme promoter pledge is **data-screenable in principle but currently a confirmed gap** (same as Zee/PC Jeweller/Vakrangee — the fourth case pointing at the same missing pledge dataset). The specific revenue-recognition-policy comparison against sector peers requires **reading the accounting-policy note** — filing-read-only, not a standard ratio.
- **One-line lesson:** promoter pledge nearing 100% is as close to a hard-veto-grade signal as a non-fraud-specific metric gets in this library — it recurs in 4 of 15 cases here and is entirely missing from ALPHA_RANKER's current data.

## 15. Karvy Stock Broking (2019–2020)
**Status: REGULATORY-ACTION/CHARGES-PENDING, PARTIALLY CONTESTED** — SEBI found (2019 orders) that Karvy Stock Broking misused clients' pledged securities held via Power-of-Attorney to raise loans (~Rs 2,300 crore reported [INFERENCE]) diverted to the group's real-estate arm (Karvy Realty) without client consent; NSE's own investigation corroborated misuse; SEBI barred Karvy from taking new broking clients. Some SEBI findings were later contested/modified before SAT; treat the precise final disposition as **UNKNOWN** without verification.

- **MO:** Client securities pooled via Power-of-Attorney into a single demat account, then pledged to banks/NBFCs to raise loans for the promoter's unrelated real-estate business — commingling client collateral with the broker's own/group's proprietary funding needs.
- **Ex-ante red flags:** (D) this is fundamentally a **structural/operational control failure** (PoA-enabled client-asset commingling), not a balance-sheet or earnings-quality signal — closer in kind to the PNB-Gitanjali SWIFT-CBS gap than to any of the accounting-fraud cases; (B) sudden, large short-term-borrowing growth at a pure broking entity, inconsistent with a commission-based business model (a business-model-mismatch tell, same family as Ricoh's leasing-income mismatch and Cox & Kings' forex-card-subsidiary drift); (C) related-party fund flow from the regulated broking entity to an unlisted, unrelated-line-of-business promoter entity (real estate).
- **Screenable now vs filing-read-only:** The core PoA/commingling control failure is **not visible in company-level fundamentals at all** — it requires depository-level (NSDL/CDSL) account reconciliation, entirely outside ALPHA_RANKER's data catalog and outside what an equity-analyst-style screen can ever see. The business-model-mismatch tell (broking entity with unusual debt growth) would be **data-screenable** if Karvy-style unlisted/private entities were even in the covered universe (they typically are not, since ALPHA_RANKER's universe is listed-equity-focused) — **structural scope gap**, not a data-quality gap.
- **One-line lesson:** some of the worst frauds in this library (PNB-Gitanjali, Karvy) are not company-financial-statement frauds at all — they are custodian/intermediary control failures invisible to any equity-fundamentals screen, and no amount of ratio-tuning fixes that; it needs a different data source category entirely (depository/bank-audit disclosures) that ALPHA_RANKER does not currently ingest.

### Data-screenable-NOW vs filing-read-only — summary
- **Screenable now (flags already live in `src/forensic/universe_forensic.py`):** CFO/PAT divergence, Sloan-accruals proxy, cash-conversion (CFO/OP), other-income dependence, tax-rate anomaly, receivables/inventory-growth-vs-sales (sparse coverage, per in-code caveat), Beneish SGI/TATA/DSRI/GMI components (AQI and composite M-score explicitly NOT computed — insufficient current-asset-split data), interest-coverage trend, debt/EBITDA trend, contingent-liabilities/net-worth.
- **Confirmed gaps (recur across multiple cases → highest-priority build list for the R&D/data desk):** (1) promoter pledge — zero coverage, recurs in 4+ cases; (2) credit-rating-agency action history — zero coverage, recurs in 4 cases (IL&FS/DHFL/Amtek/Kingfisher-adjacent); (3) auditor-change/resignation-for-cause events — zero coverage despite being the taxonomy's own named hard-veto item; (4) related-party-loan-as-%-of-balance-sheet / group ICD mapping — needs AR-note extraction, not in current schema.
- **Filing-read-only by nature (will never be a clean structured field):** related-party acquisition attempts (Satyam-Maytas), promoter-family litigation/disputes (PC Jeweller), unverified third-party partnership claims (Vakrangee), MCA/ROC address-matching between shell entities and promoters (DHFL), and anything requiring physical/asset-existence verification (Ricoh's fictitious leased assets).
- **Outside equity-fundamentals scope entirely:** bank/depository-level control failures (PNB-Gitanjali SWIFT-CBS gap, Karvy PoA-commingling) — these need a banking/depository-audit data source, not an equity forensic screen, and no amount of company-ratio tuning will surface them.

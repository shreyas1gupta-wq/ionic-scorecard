# 07 — Framework: MICROCAP lens (beyond the top 750 — a different game)

**Character:** an exclusive model, NOT a re-weighted large-cap framework. The edge source is **mispricing from neglect** (no analyst coverage, low float, information asymmetry). The dominant risk is **fraud / governance / liquidity**, so the model is **avoid-landmines-first**: forensics and promoter analysis outweigh everything, quant is supportive, and liquidity constrains sizing. Judgment-dominant with a forensic gate that is close to veto-heavy. Updated **semi-annual + event-driven** (bulk deals, results, disclosures).

**Universe:** Indian listed names **beyond the top 750** by market cap (screen out shells, suspended, ASM/GSM stage-2, penny/illiquid below a hard ADV floor).

## Why a separate model
- Sell-side estimates barely exist → estimate-revision factors are thin; you rely on primary reading (annual reports, RHPs, concalls, filings).
- Fraud base-rate is far higher → forensics is the first gate, not an overlay.
- Liquidity is the binding constraint → position sizing and impact cost are part of the score, not an afterthought.
- The winners are **operating-leverage / capacity-expansion inflections** and **neglected quality** re-rating as coverage arrives.

## The five pillars (per brief: base, promoter, business model, value, growth — plus forensics)
### 1. Promoter & Governance (heaviest weight — mostly veto/penalty)
- Promoter background, track record, other group companies (round-tripping / value-leakage across the group).
- **Pledge** (trend and level), promoter holding changes, dilution/warrant issuance to promoters at a discount.
- Related-party transactions, loans to promoters/group, related-party sales/purchases as % of revenue.
- Auditor quality (Big-N vs unknown small firm), resignations, qualifications; board independence.
- Insider / bulk / block deals, SAST/PIT disclosures — is smart money accumulating or exiting?

### 2. Business model (understandability)
- Can you explain how it makes money in two sentences? Neglect ≠ complexity — avoid black boxes.
- Customer concentration, supplier concentration, cyclicality, moat (even a small local moat), pricing power.
- Working-capital cycle (receivables/inventory days trend — the classic microcap fraud tell).

### 3. Base & Accumulation (technical, low float)
- Long base formation, low-volatility accumulation, breakout from multi-year base on volume/delivery.
- Float analysis (free float, promoter+institutional holding), liquidity/ADV, impact cost → sizing cap.
- Stage analysis; avoid vertical/parabolic already-discovered names.

### 4. Growth & Operating leverage (the upside engine)
- Capacity expansion / capex → revenue lag → margin inflection (the microcap multibagger pattern).
- Size of opportunity vs tiny base (small absolute numbers can grow fast); order book; new product/geography.
- Debt used for productive capex vs plugging losses.

### 5. Value / Mispricing (the entry)
- Cheap vs earnings/asset/replacement value BECAUSE of neglect, not because of a broken business (distinguish the two — this is the whole skill).
- Margin of safety is larger here (illiquidity + fraud risk demand it).
- Re-rating trigger: coverage initiation, index/MF eligibility, promoter/institutional buying, earnings surprise.

## Forensic gate (near-veto — see `08`)
Microcap runs the **full forensic battery as a gate, not an overlay**: Beneish M-score, CFO-vs-PAT multi-year divergence, receivables/inventory vs revenue, capex-vs-depreciation gold-plating, CWIP that never capitalizes, contingent liabilities, auditor red flags, related-party bloat. A serious flag caps the score deeply or vetoes — the asymmetry (one fraud wipes out ten winners) justifies extreme conservatism.

## Scoring specifics (microcap)
- Weight prior: Growth 0.25, Value 0.20, Quality 0.20, Promoter/Forensic 0.15+gate, Momentum/Base 0.10, Catalyst/Flow 0.05, sizing/liquidity as a hard modifier.
- Liquidity is a **score modifier and a sizing cap** — a great score on an untradeable name yields a small position, stated explicitly.
- Calibration is data-thin → wider uncertainty bands, more reliance on the forensic gate and analog reasoning; be honest about small-n and coverage gaps (no silent fallback — ask the human, brief Q17).

## Thesis paragraph style (microcap)
Primary-research flavoured: what the business is, why it's mispriced (the neglect), the promoter/governance verdict, the growth trigger, and — prominently — the **kill-risks** (governance/liquidity/customer-concentration). Exit triggers weighted toward governance/forensic breaks and thesis-confirmation-failure.

## Sizing & discipline
- Hard ADV/impact-cost cap on position size; limit-order-or-skip (firm D-031: no-fill = drop, never assume fills in dead markets).
- Basket approach (own many small positions) to survive the fraud base-rate; no single microcap sized like a large-cap conviction.

# 05 — Framework: 1-YEAR lens (blend — the earnings-revision + re-rating horizon)

**Character:** the middle horizon where earnings trajectory, estimate revisions, valuation re-rating, and 12-1 relative strength all fire together. Balanced quant + fundamental. Updated **semi-annually (Dec/Jul)** with a **quarterly track** around results. Devil's-advocate pass **mandatory**.

**Universe:** NIFTY 750.

## Factor library
### Growth & Earnings (high weight)
- Forward earnings growth trajectory (next 4–8 quarters), acceleration/deceleration.
- **Estimate-revision breadth & momentum** — the single most reliable 1Y signal (Chan-Jegadeesh-Lakonishok): net upgrades, magnitude, dispersion tightening.
- Revenue growth durability, volume vs price-led growth, order book / pipeline (for capex names), same-store/segment growth.
- Margin trajectory (operating leverage, input-cost cycle turn).

### Valuation (high weight — re-rating is the 1Y alpha)
- PE / PB / EV-EBITDA / PEG vs **own 5–10y history percentile** AND vs sector peers.
- Earnings-yield vs bond-yield gap; FCF yield.
- Re-rating potential: gap between current multiple and justified multiple given growth+quality (a cheap-but-improving name is the sweet spot).

### Momentum / Relative strength (medium weight)
- **12-1 month momentum** (the academic cross-sectional winner) and residual momentum (beta/sector-neutralized).
- Relative strength vs sector and index; stage analysis (Weinstein) for trend confirmation.

### Quality (medium weight)
- ROCE/ROE trend and *level vs cost of capital*, not a cutoff.
- FCF conversion, accruals (low accruals = higher quality earnings), debt trend / net-debt-to-EBITDA direction.
- Earnings stability & predictability.

### Management / Guidance (medium weight)
- Guidance credibility & promise-vs-delivery tracking across quarters (concall rubric, `08`).
- Capital-allocation signals (capex, buyback, dividend, M&A discipline).

### Forensic / Risk (penalty)
- Full `08` overlay; severity regime-scaled.

### Sector / Cascade (medium)
- Sector cycle position, tailwind/headwind from `03`.

## Scoring specifics (1Y)
- Weight prior: Value 0.25, Growth 0.20, Quality 0.15, Momentum 0.15, Sentiment 0.05, Catalyst 0.10, Forensic 0.10. # not fixed but dynamic
- **Regime dependence:** in a cheap-market/easing regime, Value & Growth up-weighted (re-rating fuel); in expensive/tightening, Quality & low-accruals up-weighted (defense). Down-market/high-risk regime → raise Quality weight (per brief Q8), but **guard against the value-trap and disruption traps** below.
- Calibrate score→P on **12-month forward returns**, regime-conditioned. Track quarterly for drift; re-baseline Dec/Jul.

## The two traps this lens must actively avoid (brief Q8)
1. **Quality-without-growth value trap:** a high-ROCE, no-growth, richly-valued compounder can underperform for years. Down-weight "quality" when growth is stalling AND the multiple is elevated.
2. **Quality-with-structural-change trap:** a historically great franchise facing permanent disruption (AI compressing IT-services, generic price collapse, platform shift). The cascade's *structural headwind* flag overrides the quality score.

## Thesis paragraph style (1Y)
Balanced: earnings/revision setup + valuation entry + the catalyst path to re-rating + the thesis-break trigger. One paragraph.

## Exit / review discipline (1Y)
- Full re-score Dec/Jul; quarterly checkpoint on earnings vs guidance and revision trend.
- Thesis-break triggers written at entry (e.g. "two consecutive quarters of revision downgrades + margin miss" → exit).
- Relative-strength breakdown vs sector as a technical exit overlay.

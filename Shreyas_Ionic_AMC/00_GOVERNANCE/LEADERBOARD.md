# LEADERBOARD — unbiased efficacy ranking (updated per session; settled quarterly)
**The formula is public and mechanical — no discretion in the ranking itself:**
- **Efficacy = AP earned ÷ (agent tokens ÷ 10,000)** — AlphaPoints per 10k tokens. Rewards decision-value per unit cost, so a haiku-tier data check can beat an opus-tier memo. Founding/appreciation bonuses are EXCLUDED from efficacy (they're gifts, not output).
- Raw AP shown alongside (career total). Chairs/verdict roles score via the decisions they enable (an IC chair earns when the firm later confirms the ruling was right — deferred scoring).
- Anti-gaming: AP only attaches to artifacts filed in the repo (WORK_LOG policy); Red-Team catches must be CONFIRMED (a refuted "catch" scores −10); token figures come from harness usage, not self-report.

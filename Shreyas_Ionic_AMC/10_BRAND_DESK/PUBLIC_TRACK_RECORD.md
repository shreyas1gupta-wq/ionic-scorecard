# PUBLIC TRACK RECORD — pre-registered public calls
*The credibility asset. Created 2026-07-15. Governed by `BRAND_CHARTER.md` §6.*

**Purpose:** a timestamped, auditable record of every falsifiable public claim Shreyas makes, registered BEFORE it is posted. Over 3-5 years this becomes the thing that separates him from everyone else with opinions: **receipts.**

## Rules
1. **Register before you post.** No entry added after the fact.
2. **The git commit that adds the entry IS the timestamp.** Record the hash. Do not back-date or edit a matured entry's original claim — corrections go in a new dated line, the original stands.
3. **Only falsifiable claims enter the ledger.** A market view with a horizon and a testable outcome = ledger. A pure opinion/principle post = NOT ledgered (post it, tag it opinion, move on).
4. **Score honestly at maturity. Publish hits AND misses** (quarterly, via the P7 check-in). A buried miss destroys more credibility than an open one ever could.
5. Never let a claim here breach charter §7 (no stock calls, no employer info, etc.). A claim that can only be stated by breaking §7 does not get made.

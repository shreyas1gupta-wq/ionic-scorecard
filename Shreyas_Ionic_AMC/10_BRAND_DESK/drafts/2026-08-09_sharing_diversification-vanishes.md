The diversification you count on has a habit of vanishing exactly when you need it.

I've seen it enough times to stop trusting the calm-weather version. On a normal day a portfolio looks nicely spread out — different sectors, different assets, all doing their own thing. Then a real shock hits and it all moves together, downward. Correlations snap to one, and the hedges that looked clever turn out to be the same bet wearing different clothes.

So I've started asking a narrower question about any portfolio, including my own. Not "is this diversified," but "how much of this is secretly the same trade — and does the spread hold when it actually matters."

Hoping for the best is easy. I'd rather be built for the worst.

Views are my own.

A habit I've been trying to build: deciding how much I'm willing to lose before I let myself think about how much I could make.

It's the opposite of how most of us are wired. The upside is the fun part — the story, the target, the multiple. The downside is boring, so we skip it, and end up sizing positions on optimism.

Starting from the loss changes everything downstream. How big any single position is allowed to get. How much of the book is really one bet. What I'll do when it goes against me — decided while I'm calm, not while I'm bleeding.

None of this predicts the market. It just makes sure I'm still standing when I'm finally right.

Views are my own.

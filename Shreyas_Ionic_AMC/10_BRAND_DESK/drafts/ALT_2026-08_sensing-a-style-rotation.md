Been noticing something in how conviction moves through the market.

For a while, the loudest returns came from momentum — buy what's already moving, don't ask too many questions about the price you're paying. It works, right up until the music slows and the same names that felt unstoppable start giving back years of gains in weeks.

I've been shifting my own attention toward quality and reasonable valuation instead. Not because momentum is wrong. Because it demands a kind of timing I don't trust myself to always get right, and quality doesn't ask for that. It doesn't need the story to keep working. It just needs the business to keep working.

None of this is a call on any stock or sector. It's more a comment on temperament, mine specifically. I'd rather be a little early into boring and still be able to sleep, than perfectly timed into exciting and lose the ability to.

Views are my own.

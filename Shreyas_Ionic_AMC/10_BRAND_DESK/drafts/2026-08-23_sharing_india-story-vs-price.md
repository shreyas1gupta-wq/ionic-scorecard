I hold two thoughts about Indian equities that sound contradictory, and I've made peace with both.

One: the long-term story is as strong as anything I've studied — the growth, the formalization, the runway ahead. It's hard not to be structurally optimistic.

Two: a great story and a good price are different things, and the market loves to charge you for the first while pretending it's the second.

Most of the mistakes I see — and the ones I've made — come from collapsing the two. Buying a wonderful long-term narrative at a price that already assumes it plays out perfectly. Being bullish on India and being careful about what you pay for it were never in tension. Holding both at once is, to me, most of the game.

Views are my own.

## Draft text (LinkedIn)

Something I've been sitting with lately.

After a big run, the instinct is to go hunting for the next big winner. I've caught myself doing the opposite — asking less about what could go up the most, and more about what would hurt the least if I've got it wrong.

It sounds defensive. I think it's just honest. The market rarely hands you obvious bargains. Most of the time it hands you a few fair prices and a lot of froth dressed up as opportunity, and telling those two apart is most of the job.

I'd rather hold a handful of businesses I actually understand, at prices that leave me room to be wrong, than chase the story everyone's already paying up for.

Not a call on anything. Just where my head is right now.

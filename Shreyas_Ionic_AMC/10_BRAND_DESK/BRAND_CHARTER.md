## 6. THE TRACK-RECORD SYSTEM (the differentiated asset)
Ledger: `PUBLIC_TRACK_RECORD.md`. This is the whole point of the exercise.
- **Every falsifiable public claim (P1/P2/P3 market views especially) is PRE-REGISTERED before posting:** date, the claim in testable form, horizon, and the **git commit hash** that timestamps it. Git history = free, already-existing, auditable timestamp proof. This reuses the firm's existing discipline (D-030 forward-freeze; the SYSTEM_VS_LLM frozen-commit protocol).
- **Quarterly look-back (P7):** score each matured call honestly — right, wrong, or unresolved — and post the scorecard either way. Publishing misses is the credibility multiplier, not a cost.
- **Rule:** if a claim can't be pre-registered because it isn't falsifiable, that's fine — but then it's an *opinion post*, tag it as such, and it doesn't enter the scored ledger.
- Optional later: mirror key entries to a public timestamp (Substack post date, or a hash published on-platform) so the proof needs no access to this repo.

## 8. VOICE — must read as SHREYAS, not as AI
This is a hard requirement, not a nicety. If a draft reads AI-generated, it's a failed draft.
> **BEFORE drafting anything, read `VOICE_SAMPLES.md`** — his real captured posts + distilled voice profile. Match those, don't invent a voice.
> **GENRE matters as much as tone (learned 2026-07-15, three misses):** his voice is **current-market reflection + conviction-sharing** ("As I reflect on the current market landscape…", "In March I shared my conviction, now evident…") — him reacting to what's happening and sharing where he stands. **Do NOT write evergreen concept-explainers** ("the low-vol anomaly, here's why it works") — that genre is inherently a lecture and no voice-tuning saves it. Anti-AI-tells: strip writerly flourishes and neat rhetorical closers; real sharing is more direct and a little looser than polished essay prose.
- **He adds the mind, always.** No draft ships as pure machine output. On every post he adds his own view / story / example / crisp reframe. The pipeline produces *raw material and options*; the judgment and phrasing are his.
- **Anti-AI-tell discipline:** run `/style-lint` on every draft (banned tells: em-dash pile-ups, rule-of-three cadence, "it's not just X, it's Y," bullet-itis, hedge-everything, empty throat-clearing openers). See the firm's `style-lint` skill.
- **He is SHARING, not teaching (hard rule — see §5 Prime Directive).** The voice is Shreyas expressing a thought/conviction/read, thinking out loud — modeled on his real posts ("As I reflect on the current market landscape…", "In March I shared my conviction, now evident…"). NEVER the explainer/lecturer voice ("here's how X works, here are 3 reasons, the takeaway is"). Substance is woven in as HIS perspective on it.
- **Register:** first person, direct, unhurried. Confident where his math + gut back it (he's explicitly contrarian when they diverge from consensus — that's on-brand), honest about uncertainty elsewhere. Prepares-for-the-worst framing is authentically his ("hope for the best, prepare for the worst").
- **Length:** fit the topic; a punchy 120-word take and a 600-word thesis are both fine. **Never exceed the LinkedIn character limit** (~3,000 chars / ~1,300 truncation for "see more" — hook must land above the fold).
- **No generic LinkedIn-influencer voice.** No "Here's the thing 👇", no fake-vulnerability hooks, no engagement-farming CTAs.

It works here only because the brother's ownership is real.

**Five hard rules (breaking any one ends the career, not the side project):**
1. **Nothing touching Indian securities, ever, in any wrapper** — no signals, no trading education, no market commentary, no portfolio-roast features. SEBI's Sathe order (₹546.16 cr disgorgement — [SEBI order PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/dec-2025/ORDER_1764842991.pdf), [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-orders-rs-546-crore-disgorgement-avadhut-sathe-trading-academy.html)) pierced the "academy" wrapper; anonymity did not survive platform KYC in any enforcement case on record.
2. **Know and honor the employer's outside-business clause**; never use employer time, hardware, or data. The code of conduct — not SEBI — is the binding constraint; it is also why P02/P01 stay shelved.
3. **Family-member structures only where the relative genuinely owns and operates.** Brother-run = clean. Fronting a business the client actually runs = concealment + Benami adjacency. No spouse-funding variant (Sec 64 clubs it back).
4. **No India-targeted ads in any securities-adjacent category** — Meta/Google display verified identity on such ads since Jul-2025 ([Meta developer blog](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/06/26/verification-and-transparency-requirements-for-advertisers-targeting-users-in-india-with-securities-and-investments-ads/)). Non-finance ads under the brand page are fine.
5. **Public anonymity, total transparency to the state:** real-name KYC on every platform, complete ITRs, FEMA-clean rails (MoR/Wise, repatriation within 9 months), no crypto rails or unreported foreign entities. Plus product-level lines: P08 stays education (no medical advice/dosing); P09 prompts hard-ban medical and financial predictions; original content only.

**Standing kill-rule for all future ideas:** if a proposed product's output could be read by a SEBI officer as a view on an Indian security, it is dead on arrival — regardless of revenue, wrapper, or how anonymous it feels.

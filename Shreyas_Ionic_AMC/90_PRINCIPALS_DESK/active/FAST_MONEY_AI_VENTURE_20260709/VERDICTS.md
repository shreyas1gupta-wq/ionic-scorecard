# Regulatory & Employer-Risk Attack — Verdict Report

## The single biggest thing the brief got wrong

**"Route it through a family-member entity — it makes the trail genuinely someone else's." This is false and dangerous.** Three reasons, all verifiable: (1) SEBI demonstrably pierces exactly this structure — in its 2025-26 Telegram-channel order (Intraday Jackpot / PDTI case) SEBI traced Rigi/Cosmofeed platform KYC, PAN, UPI IDs, and beneficiary bank accounts, ordered a ₹9.02 cr refund, **and separately penalized Beauti Kumari ₹10 lakh purely for "facilitating"** — the fronting family member gets charged too ([Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/902-crore-refund-40-lakh-penalty-sebi-bars-3-telegram-channel-operators/80354.html), [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-sebi-unregistered-advisor-telegram-social-media-scrutiny-complaint/)). (2) Employer codes of conduct bind his **conduct**, not the entity's letterhead: broking/AMC dealing codes (per the SEBI-mandated framework, [SEBI guidelines](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/CIR04MF2001_h.html)) require declaration/preclearance of outside business activity by the employee. If he does the work, fronting converts a disclosure lapse into **active concealment** — far worse at termination or enforcement time. (3) If he funds it, operates it, and enjoys the money while a relative holds title, that fact pattern brushes the Benami Transactions Act, not just Section 64. Fronting is acceptable ONLY where the relative genuinely owns and runs the business (the brother-run NEET-PG play qualifies; a "brother-fronted" trading-indicator shop does not).

## Per-play verdicts

**Paid signals / Telegram calls / India trading education / algo-marketplace (all lanes' declared dead ends): CAREER-RISK-KILL — concur, and the briefs understate the mechanism.** Rule: SEBI (Research Analysts) Regs 2014 + IA Regs (any consideration for security-specific advice), Jan-29-2025 Sec-16A circular (3-month data lag, no named securities), Feb-2025 algo circular (black-box strategy provision = mandatory RA registration + exchange empanelment). How he's caught: platform KYC subpoena (Rigi/Cosmofeed/Whop all KYC the payee), PAN-linked payment trails, and SEBI's stated 70k+ takedown program. As an intermediary employee he faces the SEBI order **plus** certain termination **plus** industry blacklisting.

**TradingView indicator suite (fin lane Play 1): downgrade from "best risk/reward" to SAFE-WITH-GUARDRAILS, borderline KILL.** The fin-adjacent lane and the competitor-teardown lane directly contradict each other here; the teardown lane ("AVOID first — closest thing to unregistered advice") is right. An indicator that paints entries/exits/"buy zones" IS a recommendation in SEBI's Sathe/algo-circular logic if Indian customers buy it — and TradingView is heavily used by Indians; he cannot control who subscribes. TradingView's vendor rules require no public identity ([vendor requirements](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000549951-vendor-requirements/) — confirmed, no ID/real-name rule) but off-platform payment (Whop/Gumroad) KYCs him, and moderators require public scripts + signatures that accumulate a discoverable footprint. Guardrails if attempted anyway: analytics/visualization only (no arrow-on-chart signals), hard geo-refusal of Indian customers in ToS + checkout, no India-targeted marketing, zero performance claims, and **employer disclosure per his code of conduct** — which most broking compliance desks will refuse for anything trading-adjacent. Realistically that last guardrail is fatal; treat this play as KILL unless his code of conduct genuinely permits it.

**AI trading journal SaaS (fin lane Play 2, teardown lane (a)): SAFE-WITH-GUARDRAILS — the only survivable finance play.** Retrospective analysis of the user's own executed trades is not "research services" under the RA definition; the Jul-23-2025 SEBI FAQ carve-out for general communications supports this ([SEBI FAQ circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/jul-2025/1753269723942.pdf)). Guardrails: (i) no forward-looking module ever — no "what to trade," no screeners framed as picks, no signal add-on (one feature flag away from RA territory); (ii) no India-targeted Meta ads — Meta's securities-ads policy is category-broad and even the "exempt" path publicly displays payer/beneficiary name and location ([Meta developer blog](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/06/26/verification-and-transparency-requirements-for-advertisers-targeting-users-in-india-with-securities-and-investments-ads/)) — anonymity dies either way; (iii) the employer-conflict problem is REAL even here: Angel One itself ships trade analytics; building a competing product while employed is a conflict his code likely captures. Non-India customers first.

**Backtesting course / quant-methods newsletter: SAFE-WITH-GUARDRAILS.** Legal under the Jan-2025 circular ONLY with ≥3-month-lagged data, no named-security forward statements, no performance claims. Same Meta-ads trap as above. Drift risk to market views is the named failure mode — agree with the brief.

**Astrology AI, wedding-media AI, micro-drama, English practice, global utility apps, GLP-1 tracker, subscription auditor, Chrome extensions: SAFE.** Zero SEBI surface. Residual guardrails: prompt-level ban on financial/medical predictions (astro), no remedy/cure claims (Drugs & Magic Remedies Act touches advertised "solutions"), "informational not diagnostic" (GLP-1; no dosing), and the C11 app-store identity fix (org/brother account or web-first). Note: even non-finance side business technically requires whatever outside-business declaration his employment contract specifies — a non-finance disclosure is usually approvable; a finance one is not. That asymmetry should drive lane choice.

**Brother-fronted NEET-PG system: SAFE — the cleanest structure in the brief,** because the brother genuinely operates it (Section 64 confirmed: clubbing covers spouse/minor/son's wife/HUF only — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/section-64-clubbing-income)). Guardrails: original content only (Marrow-derived decks = copyright claim against the brother), gift deed documented, brother files ITR.

## Corrected numbers

## Discovery-vector summary (how he'd actually get caught)

Ranked by likelihood: (1) payment-platform KYC/PAN trail under subpoena or SEBI examination (proven method, Telegram order); (2) GST registration under his PAN once turnover crosses ₹20L; (3) Meta/Google ad-account verification — any India-targeted securities-adjacent ad displays identity publicly; (4) app-store legal-name display (Apple individual / Play merchant — confirmed); (5) colleague/whistleblower recognition of domain expertise or writing style; (6) ITR/AIS if employer policy demands income declaration. EPFO/UAN and MCA checks miss a proprietorship — the anon-ops lane is correct there. FEMA/tax mechanics in the brief (44AD, LUT, 9-month realization, no crypto rails, no unreported US LLC) are sound.

**Net compliance ranking of the brief's plays:** non-finance apps (astro/wedding/GLP-1/utility) > brother-run med-ed > trading journal (guardrailed) > backtesting course > TradingView indicators (borderline kill) > anything signals-shaped (kill). The brief's overall SEBI map is accurate; its two errors are the family-fronting immunity myth and underweighting that the employer code of conduct — not SEBI — decides whether even the "safe" finance plays are viable.

# SATURATION & MOAT ATTACK — Verdict Report (2026-07-09)

## The single biggest thing the brief got wrong

**Vintage bias.** Every named success story started in a different supply regime: LuxAlgo (2019), Easlo (2021), HabitKit/Roehl (2022, 2.5 yrs to $10K MRR), Cal AI (2023), KundliGPT/AstroSage-AI (2023-24), AnkiHub (2021). Meanwhile the brief's own data shows supply exploded ~7x since then: [14,700 new subscription apps/month by Jan 2026 vs 2,000 in Jan 2022](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026/) and [145,450 new Whop products in 2025 alone (7x prior year)](https://whoptrends.com/blog/whop-creator-earnings-data-2026). The brief quotes 2023-24 playbooks as live windows; several are verifiably shut. Second systemic error: at least four "gap" claims were stale on publication (trading-journal AI, GLP-1 lateness, wedding-invite arbitrage, astrology openness).

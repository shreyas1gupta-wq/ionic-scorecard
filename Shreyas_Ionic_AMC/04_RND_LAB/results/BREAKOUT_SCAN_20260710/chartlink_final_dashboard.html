What the grid proves

1. The 1.5×ATR initial stop is the robust upgrade. It ranks top-tier with every trail pairing (+43% to +49%). Volatility-adjusted stops cut losers faster without more false stops — average loss shrinks from −74k to −53k.

2. A slow trail (EMA20 / 20DMA) adds value; a fast trail destroys it. All five KC-upper-1.5ATR cells are the bottom five of the whole grid. Demanding price stay above the upper band exits everything in ~7 days.

3. Capital recycling is the hidden engine. Per-trade expectancy is highest with no trail — but the portfolio is cash-constrained (147 skips at baseline). Exits that free capital a week earlier take 12-19 more signals, and that dominates.

4. Overfitting warning. 35 variants on one 8-month window: treat the +49% cell as the top of a noisy distribution. The robust conclusion is the family pattern, not the single cell. Paper-trade spec: SL = entry − 1.5×ATR14, trail = close < EMA20 (from day 2), max hold 30 trading days, 10% entries, first-come-first-served.

## A. Firm-earned lessons (paid for with real mistakes — never re-learn)
1. **VRP is the meta-edge in Indian options:** implied > realized persistently → selling wins, buying loses. Every profitable option sleeve we have is short-vol; every buying family died (K-001, K-004).
2. **Measurement artifacts we have personally produced** (Red Team trophy wall): return-on-net-debit denominator (FF "+80%"); spreading trade P&L across holding days (Sharpe 7-10, Kelly 300); monthly-compounded sleeve CAGRs (+246%/+681% "annualized" fantasies); near-expiry return-on-premium explosions (+357% earnings "wins"); partial-year data reading as "positive every year". Antidotes: exit-period booking, stable denominators (premium/spot), per-trade edge as the headline metric, periods-per-year coverage checks.
3. **Lookahead in stock selection:** any filter built from realized outcomes (the "16 landmines") is untradeable. Live filters must be ex-ante: IV at entry (corr −0.23 with future worst-case), liquidity/OI, event calendar, walk-forward-proven history rules only for stocks that HAVE history.
4. **Tails are unforecastable at trade level; survivable at portfolio level.** DMA/vol/downside filters, stops (gap through), and bought wings (theta bleed + unpriceable strikes) all FAILED to cap the FF tail. What works: small size × many concurrent idiosyncratic positions, inverse-IV sizing, staggered entries (April-2026 one-day cluster = correlated blowup), event gates.
5. **Cap-tier gating is strategy-specific:** premium harvesting (IV/RV, strangle) IMPROVES on mid-caps (richer IV) with sizing discipline; structure/liquidity-sensitive strategies (calendars) and binary-event strategies (earnings) degrade — large-cap only.
6. **Event gates are the cheapest tail insurance:** IT earnings gap −31..−47% through a short straddle; banks cluster on one Saturday; defence order flow and FDA letters are un-calendarable → sector analysts own the calendars, desk trades AROUND them.
7. **Data coverage is alpha:** expanding 88→210 F&O names (free NSE bhavcopy) roughly doubled every strategy's sample and surfaced fresh tradeables. Check coverage before inventing signals.
8. **DENOMINATOR DISEASE — three strikes, now a HARD RULE:** FF v1 (net-debit), S-02 (decaying leg premium), S-03 (back-leg premium). ANY per-trade return must ALSO be reported in denominator-free RUPEE POINTS and % of SPOT before it may be believed; a strategy whose edge changes sign between denominators is an artifact. (purgedcv units trap is the same family: bars_per_year mis-set silently inflates DSR.)
9. **The pre-IC incremental-shuffle (Gate-5 SOP) kills fictions cheaply:** first two uses re-priced S-01 (+37.6%→+11.4 incremental) and KILLED S-02 pre-IC (+21.6%→ +4.8% fragile crush; unconditional short-vol beat it). `c4_short_thru` in stock_earnings_vol.parquet is CONTAMINATED (per-leg decaying denominator) — never consume it raw.
9. **NEVER settle a trade at a date beyond max(available data)** — `spot.asof(future_expiry)` returns a stale price and FABRICATES settlement wins (S-04: 84 future-expiry rows, 100% fake hit). Guard L7 added. Strategy-specific physical bounds beat generic thresholds: a short strangle cannot earn more than its premium (~3-4% spot); 380 rows violated physics and passed a 60% generic guard.
10. **Angel purges expired option contracts** — capture before expiry (15:45 task) or lose the data forever. NSE archives (nsearchives) DO work through the corporate proxy; only some /api endpoints are blocked.

14. **Circuit/volume-conditional fills (Principal rule, 2026-07-04):** fixed-bps slippage lies precisely where momentum trades — entry days cluster with upper circuits, stop days with lower circuits, and fills on locked/thin bars are fabrications. Standing rule: `lib/execution_realism.fill_check()` in every equity backtest (no-fill on locked/zero-volume bars, 2x/3x slippage on thin bars). BT-11 v1/union runs did NOT have this — v1.5 must; expect a further haircut on the momentum edge (that is the honest direction).
15. **Random-basket benchmark law (D-029):** the honest null for a stock-selection strategy is not an index (cap-weighted, cost-free) but the DISTRIBUTION of cost-loaded random baskets from the same segment at the same position count. The mean of 10,000 random EW baskets converges near the segment EW average minus cost drag — the PERCENTILE BANDS are the real information (a strategy at the 60th percentile of random is luck; 95th+ is selection). Standing series in datasets/derived/benchmarks_random/. Segment PIT approximation: Large=N100 (N50+Next50), Mid=N200 minus N100, Small=N500 minus N200 (stated approximation of NSE's 1-100/101-250/251-500 mcap ranks).
16. **Rebalance cadence IS part of the edge (D-029 factor family):** monthly rebalancing of momentum-quality factor portfolios = 330-450% annual one-way turnover -> 3.5-10.7pp/yr cost drag at honest smallcap slippage; NSE runs these indices semiannually FOR A REASON. Factor survives; the vehicle cadence decides if YOU capture it.

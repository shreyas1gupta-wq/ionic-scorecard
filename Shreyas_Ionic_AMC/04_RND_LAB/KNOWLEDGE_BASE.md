## A. Firm-earned lessons (paid for with real mistakes — never re-learn)
1. **VRP is the meta-edge in Indian options:** implied > realized persistently → selling wins, buying loses. Every profitable option sleeve we have is short-vol; every buying family died (K-001, K-004).
2. **Measurement artifacts we have personally produced** (Red Team trophy wall): return-on-net-debit denominator (FF "+80%"); spreading trade P&L across holding days (Sharpe 7-10, Kelly 300); monthly-compounded sleeve CAGRs (+246%/+681% "annualized" fantasies); near-expiry return-on-premium explosions (+357% earnings "wins"); partial-year data reading as "positive every year". Antidotes: exit-period booking, stable denominators (premium/spot), per-trade edge as the headline metric, periods-per-year coverage checks.
3. **Lookahead in stock selection:** any filter built from realized outcomes (the "16 landmines") is untradeable. Live filters must be ex-ante: IV at entry (corr −0.23 with future worst-case), liquidity/OI, event calendar, walk-forward-proven history rules only for stocks that HAVE history.
4. **Tails are unforecastable at trade level; survivable at portfolio level.** DMA/vol/downside filters, stops (gap through), and bought wings (theta bleed + unpriceable strikes) all FAILED to cap the FF tail. What works: small size × many concurrent idiosyncratic positions, inverse-IV sizing, staggered entries (April-2026 one-day cluster = correlated blowup), event gates.
5. **Cap-tier gating is strategy-specific:** premium harvesting (IV/RV, strangle) IMPROVES on mid-caps (richer IV) with sizing discipline; structure/liquidity-sensitive strategies (calendars) and binary-event strategies (earnings) degrade — large-cap only.
6. **Event gates are the cheapest tail insurance:** IT earnings gap −31..−47% through a short straddle; banks cluster on one Saturday; defence order flow and FDA letters are un-calendarable → sector analysts own the calendars, desk trades AROUND them.
7. **Data coverage is alpha:** expanding 88→210 F&O names (free NSE bhavcopy) roughly doubled every strategy's sample and surfaced fresh tradeables. Check coverage before inventing signals.
8. **DENOMINATOR DISEASE — three strikes, now a HARD RULE:** FF v1 (net-debit), S-02 (decaying leg premium), S-03 (back-leg premium). ANY per-trade return must ALSO be reported in denominator-free RUPEE POINTS and % of SPOT before it may be believed; a strategy whose edge changes sign between denominators is an artifact. (purgedcv units trap is the same family: bars_per_year mis-set silently inflates DSR.)
9. **The pre-IC incremental-shuffle (Gate-5 SOP) kills fictions cheaply:** first two uses re-priced S-01 (+37.6%→+11.4 incremental) and KILLED S-02 pre-IC (+21.6%→ +4.8% fragile crush; unconditional short-vol beat it). `c4_short_thru` in stock_earnings_vol.parquet is CONTAMINATED (per-leg decaying denominator) — never consume it raw.
9. **NEVER settle a trade at a date beyond max(available data)** — `spot.asof(future_expiry)` returns a stale price and FABRICATES settlement wins (S-04: 84 future-expiry rows, 100% fake hit). Guard L7 added. Strategy-specific physical bounds beat generic thresholds: a short strangle cannot earn more than its premium (~3-4% spot); 380 rows violated physics and passed a 60% generic guard.
10. **Angel purges expired option contracts** — capture before expiry (15:45 task) or lose the data forever. NSE archives (nsearchives) DO work through the corporate proxy; only some /api endpoints are blocked.
14. **Edge in dead markets is not tradeable edge; run the fill-rate audit BEFORE the sizing/sensitivity debate (S-03/K-012, 2026-07-05):** a signal can be placebo-real (FF at the 100th pctile vs matched nulls) and still untradeable -- 61% of the calendar's forward signals fired into back-leg markets with zero volume and mostly zero OI (even mega-caps 100% drop). We burned a full parameter-sensitivity leg tuning premium-caps on a strategy where most trades cannot be filled at all. SEQUENCE: fillability/existence check -> then sizing -> then parameter sensitivity. A sizing cap of any width cannot fix a zero.
15. **Ex-ante liquidity gates can ADMIT weaker trades -- always test gate-vs-drop:** the intuitive fix (require back-leg liquidity before the signal fires) moved entries onto later, weaker leads that LOSE money; dropping-and-booking-0 (+3.88/Rs100) beat gating-and-entering-worse (+0.99). A liquidity filter is not free -- it changes WHICH trades you take, not just whether you can fill them. Pre-register the gate-vs-drop comparison as a kill criterion.
16. **v1 -> v2 script rewrites can INJECT lookahead -- diff legacy engines as an audit surface:** forward_factor v1 used a causal earliest-FF-cross entry; v2 silently switched to a non-causal argmax-FF-over-lookback entry (T9-class), inflating the number by an amount invisible in the stored artifact. A rewrite is a lookahead-injection risk even when no change is intended. Diffing successive engine versions is now part of the D-028 lookahead-audit surface.
17. **The entry-fill convention (same-day-close vs D+1) must be pre-registered -- it alone swings ~1pp/Rs100:** causal+gated+same-day = +0.99 vs causal+gated+D+1 = -0.03 (the pre-registered verdict). Fill timing is a first-order result driver, not a detail; freeze it in the spec BEFORE the run. Same-day-close is defensible only when the signal is computable intraday and orders are placeable by close -- and even then it is the OPTIMISTIC bound, never the verdict bound.
18. **Honesty-probe #1 result -- process integrity under a boss-triggered review (2026-07-05):** the kill -> Principal-challenge -> partial-reversal -> placebo-validation -> execution-audit loop self-corrected in BOTH directions and returned a pre-registered FAIL even though the Principal's review request softly signalled he hoped for a resurrection. The team also SURFACED (not buried) a new lookahead in its own supporting evidence mid-rescue, and three analysts triangulated a lost sizing script rather than trusting one reconstruction. Anti-sycophancy law: a review triggered by the boss is not a mandate to manufacture a survivor; separate "signal real" from "vehicle tradeable," and honor the pre-registered number. Kill credibility is the firm's most valuable asset.

14. **Circuit/volume-conditional fills (Principal rule, 2026-07-04):** fixed-bps slippage lies precisely where momentum trades — entry days cluster with upper circuits, stop days with lower circuits, and fills on locked/thin bars are fabrications. 15. **Random-basket benchmark law (D-029):** the honest null for a stock-selection strategy is not an index (cap-weighted, cost-free) but the DISTRIBUTION of cost-loaded random baskets from the same segment at the same position count. The mean of 10,000 random EW baskets converges near the segment EW average minus cost drag — the PERCENTILE BANDS are the real information (a strategy at the 60th percentile of random is luck; 95th+ is selection). Standing series in datasets/derived/benchmarks_random/. Segment PIT approximation: Large=N100 (N50+Next50), Mid=N200 minus N100, Small=N500 minus N200 (stated approximation of NSE's 1-100/101-250/251-500 mcap ranks).
16. **Rebalance cadence IS part of the edge (D-029 factor family):** monthly rebalancing of momentum-quality factor portfolios = 330-450% annual one-way turnover -> 3.5-10.7pp/yr cost drag at honest smallcap slippage; NSE runs these indices semiannually FOR A REASON. 20. **The turnover-matched comparator (Nikhil, I-016):** a strategy can pass EVERY statistical gate (DSR 0.9995, PBO 19.8%, plateau, lookahead 0-FAIL) and still have no selection edge — because the HURDLE churned 3x more than the strategy and paid 3x the costs. Gates test the NUMBER; the red team tests the INTERPRETATION.

22. **Post-publication decay is 50% denominator + 50% real crowding (Librarian, 2026-07-07, from LITSCAN_option_selling_meanrev_20260707):** Literature (McLean-Pontiff 2016, arXiv:2512.11913 2025) documents 26–58% post-publication decay on factor returns; VRP strategies specifically experiencing crowding post-2023. But the firm's DENOMINATOR_DISEASE catches a confound: some decay is mis-measurement (changing risk exposure as liquidity tightens; KB A.2 + A.8 + A.9 already caught this in FF, S-02, S-03), and some is real crowding (arbitrage capital + investor learning per publication). Separate via: (1) forward-test with 1.5–2.0× tighter costs than backtest; (2) compare forward Sharpe/XIRR to backtest as a decay factor (if backtest +2.0 Sharpe gross, forward <1.5 is normal; <1.0 is genuine crowding); (3) track capacity + trade count (tightening capacity or dropping win-rate with volume = real crowding).
23. **Regime filtering is not parameter tuning; it is survival insurance (Librarian, 2026-07-07):** Any mean-reversion signal (RSI, Bollinger, Z-score) applied to derivatives MUST gate on market regime (VIX, trend, breadth). Mean-reversion in trends produces negative-expectancy, and for short-option positions (unlimited loss potential) this is existential tail risk, not just a performance drag. Examples from LITSCAN_option_selling_meanrev: RSI(2) signal requires VIX filter (improves profit factor 39%); Bollinger Bands in uptrends fail systematically ("trending days produce catastrophic losing streaks without regime filters"); Z-score sell-200-OTM-calls on up-days = hidden unlimited exposure. This is not sophistication; it is existential gate that survives even if the signal is 95th-percentile backtested.
24. **Volatility-selling post-publication XIRR should be pre-registered as 50% of backtest gross; track forward (Librarian, 2026-07-07):** The 50% haircut is not pessimism — it is empirical prior from peer-reviewed literature (McLean-Pontiff on stock factors: 58% post-pub decay; VRP factors 26–40% empirically). Any short-vol / VRP backtest claiming >1.5 Sharpe net is likely under-costing slippage (most common error) or using inflated denominators. Realistic index VRP XIRR post-costs is 15–25%, Sharpe 0.9–1.2 vs 6% rf. XIRR>50% / Sharpe>2 is achievable intra-regime (April 2014, Jan 2021) but NOT as rolling 1–3 year expectation. Pre-register forward expectation at 50% of backtest gross, then track actual; if forward beats 50%, celebrate; if it lands at 50%, that's crowding decay (expected, not fault).
25a. **Sleeve correlation must be measured at the horizon where drawdowns live — daily corr on asynchronous sleeves is an artifact (CA-BOOK, 2026-07-13):** CA showed 0.00-0.02 DAILY correlation to every stacked-book sleeve, but +0.36..+0.54 MONTHLY correlation to the equity sleeves. Sleeves that trade on different days look independent at daily frequency while sharing one factor at the monthly/DD horizon. Any stacking decision, book Sharpe projection, or "uncorrelated diversifier" claim must quote monthly-horizon (or drawdown-window) correlation; daily corr systematically overstates diversification for episodic strategies. Corollary from the same card: an uncorrelated sleeve whose standalone Sharpe is below the book average CANNOT improve the frontier at DD parity — sqrt(N) stacking math only works with book-quality sleeves.
25. **Static index collars cannot armor a stock-selection book — two independent failure modes (CA-COLLAR, 2026-07-13):** (1) V-recovery whipsaw: the long put pays at the crash bottom, then the rolled short call is run over for the entire recovery — 2020 collar netted −12.2% of notional DESPITE the March put paying +17.4%; the crash payout is refunded with interest. (2) Hedge-basis mismatch: a momentum/midcap selection book's worst drawdown (CA's 2018-19 −50% grind) happens while NIFTY is flat/up — an index option never attaches to idiosyncratic/factor DD. Verified on 15yr fo_idx settles, 122/127 months, exact strikes; the overlay cut CAGR 14.1%→9.0% AND worsened maxDD 50.1%→52.4%. Implication: armor selection books with position-level exits/regime gates (which act on the actual DD source) or hedge the factor (midcap futures), not spot-index collars.

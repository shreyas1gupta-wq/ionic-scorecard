## Bull case

The clearest positive is that the FY26 loss shows a real, PIT-verified inflection rather than continued deterioration: quarterly EBITDA margin recovered for three straight quarters (5.52% Jun-25, 7.76% Sep-25, 9.15% Dec-25, 16.74% Mar-26), each figure reconciling exactly to company-reported numbers, and this tracks a genuine external tailwind rather than a cost-cutting story -- management cites US biopharma/biotech funding growth of +75% YoY in H2FY26 (+30% for FY26 overall) feeding a pickup in CDMO RFPs and order inflows after a single-client de-stocking episode. The two non-CDMO segments provide real ballast: Complex Hospital Generics holds a 47% US market share in inhalation anesthesia (up from 45% in Mar-24), a category with genuine entry barriers and few qualified global suppliers, reinforced by the Kenalog bolt-on; India Consumer Healthcare grew Power Brands 24% and e-commerce 48% (now 27% of that segment's revenue), the fastest-growing and least cyclical piece of the business. Balance-sheet discipline held through the loss year -- net debt was flat to down versus FY25 (H1FY26 net debt Rs 3,971cr, down Rs 228cr HoH) even while funding US$94mn of CDMO capacity expansion (Lexington/Riverview, US) and an acquisition, and D/E of 0.69x is not alarming for a capital-intensive CDMO/manufacturing platform. On compliance, while three sites drew Form-483s within about 14 months, none has escalated to OAI or a warning letter -- the most recent (Sellersville, US, May-2026, three observations) was explicitly recommended for VAI classification with no data-integrity findings, the mildest FDA outcome category. Finally, the promoter group (Ajay Piramal lineage, ~34.8-34.9% holding) has encumbered zero shares through the loss-making year, a clean signal of continued conviction at the controlling-shareholder level.

## Bear case

The bear case is equally real. FY26's net loss (Rs 325.94cr) reflects genuine operating deleverage, not one clean one-off -- even Q4 alone, the best quarter of the year, still logged a net loss (Rs 8.83cr) after a Rs 176cr impairment on intangibles under development, and gross profit margin compressed 369bps YoY even as EBITDA margin recovered sequentially. Interest coverage of 2.7x is thin: on Rs 5,675cr of gross borrowings, a stall in the CDMO recovery or a rate shock would bite quickly, and this is not a business with idle balance-sheet slack to absorb a second consecutive weak year. The entire recovery thesis leans on one external, historically volatile variable -- global biotech/biopharma capital-markets funding, which whipsawed from a multi-year drought in 2022-23 to the current recovery -- concentrated in a CDMO segment that is ~60% of revenue; if that funding cycle stalls again, the FY27 guidance (management's own "accelerated growth in EBITDA and PAT," one analyst model showing revenue to Rs 10,340cr and EPS turning positive to ~Rs 2.97) breaks along with it. Management's FY30 aspiration ($2bn revenue / 25% EBITDA margin / 1x net debt-to-EBITDA, first announced Sep-2024) requires roughly 17-18% revenue CAGR and ~1,400bps of margin expansion simultaneously from FY26's ~10.4% blended base -- a demanding double this same management has already partly missed once, since FY26 itself was expected to show clear recovery and instead posted a full-year loss. On compliance, three different sites in the 15+-site network (Turbhe Feb-25, Digwal Feb-26, Sellersville May-26) drew Form-483s within about 14 months -- individually mild (VAI-level, no OAI, no warning letters), but collectively a cross-site quality-system stress pattern in a network this size is a genuine, monitorable tail risk this desk's charter specifically flags plants for.

Buying TIINDIA today is largely a leveraged, indirect bet on CG Power's cycle continuing, layered with an auto-components franchise carrying real customer-concentration risk and an EV mobility unit that management itself says is financing- and charging-infrastructure constrained. If either engine cools, the multiple has little cushion.

## Detailed rationale

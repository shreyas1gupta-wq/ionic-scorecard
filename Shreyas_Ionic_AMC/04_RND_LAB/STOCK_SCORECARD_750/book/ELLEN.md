## Detailed rationale

Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases (incorporated 1973, Kolkata-based, listed June 2025) manufactures and supplies industrial, medical and speciality gases (oxygen, nitrogen, argon, CO2, acetylene, helium, hydrogen, dry ice) from nine facilities across East and South India, serving steel, pharma, defence and healthcare customers under a mix of long-duration onsite take-or-pay contracts and merchant/bulk supply. The bull case rests on capacity: Bulk capacity is set to expand from 911 to 1,131 TPD and Onsite from 698 to ~1,018 TPD over the next 12 months, led by the Uluberia-II (220 TPD) and a new East India onsite (320 TPD) plant, funded off a net-cash balance sheet still holding a large chunk of the June-2025 IPO proceeds. Gases-segment EBITDA margin did expand ~500bps YoY to 38.4% in FY26, and sell-side coverage (JM Financial TP Rs 345; Motilal Oswal TP Rs 330 on 26x FY28E EPS) models a 24-30% FY26-29E revenue/EBITDA/PAT CAGR off this capacity build.

The quant score (final_score_3y 38.2, final_score_1y 53.5, recommendation_overall Sell) is corroborated, not contradicted, by the fundamentals. FY26 revenue grew only 9.3% YoY (Rs 341.6cr vs Rs 312.5cr) -- roughly half the historical 3-year CAGR of ~18.6% -- and Q4 FY26 profit fell 12.3% QoQ (Rs 22.88cr vs Rs 26.09cr Q3) even as revenue ticked up 7.5% QoQ, with PAT margin compressing ~590bps. More important is earnings quality: other income of ~Rs 14.6cr was 43.7% of FY26 PBT (per MarketsMojo's analysis), i.e. a large share of the headline +25.4% FY26 PAT growth is treasury interest on unspent IPO cash, not core-business momentum -- a one-off-adjacent item that inflates the reported growth. Q4 also carried ~Rs 4.6cr of genuine one-offs (leave-encashment provisioning, a legacy-investment impairment, a one-time customer settlement) that are non-recurring in the other direction (profit drag, not inflation) and roughly wash out against the other-income tailwind. ROE is reported inconsistently across sources depending on the annualization window used post-IPO (CSV TTM 21.2%; Screener 14.2%; MarketsMojo Q4-annualized 9.0%) -- flagged as a data-quality note, not escalated per skill rule (stale/volatile data around a recent IPO's changing share base is normal, not a finding).

Valuation does not give room for the deceleration: the stock trades at ~40x trailing PE (39.8x per scorecard, 40.3x per Screener) and ~36x EV/EBITDA versus a ~23x industry average (Linde India, INOX Air Products), pricing in sustained mid-20s% growth the FY26 print (9.3%) has not delivered. The stock is already down ~52% over 12 months (matches CSV ret_12m -51.9%) from its July-2025 post-listing high of Rs 637 to the ~Rs 258-298 range now -- the IPO-year froth that inflated the initial growth narrative is unwinding, and the desk should not read the correction as a buying opportunity absent evidence the new capacity actually reaccelerates organic (ex-other-income) growth. Balance sheet is clean (net cash, D/E marginal per scorecard at 0.19x on a gross basis, no promoter pledge, 77.15% promoter holding as of Jun-2026), so this is a valuation/earnings-quality call, not a solvency one.

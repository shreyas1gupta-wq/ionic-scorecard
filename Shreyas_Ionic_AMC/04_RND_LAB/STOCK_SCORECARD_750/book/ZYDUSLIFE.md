# ZYDUSLIFE — Zydus Lifesciences Ltd.

> [!summary]
> Zydus had a strong FY26 on paper -- revenue up 17%, profit up 11-15%, margins expanding -- but a large part of the growth engine behind that print, generic Revlimid and generic Myrbetriq (mirabegron), is now winding down or capped by a licensing deadline in September 2027, so the reported growth rate overstates what the ongoing business can repeat. The stock, near its 52-week high at roughly 21-22 times trailing earnings, is priced about in line with the sector already, and several sell-side price targets sit below today's quote. The India branded-medicines business remains a genuine, well-run compounder, and a possible new US specialty drug (Saroglitazar, under FDA priority review with a decision due late November 2026) offers real upside if approved -- but two separate, unresolved US FDA plant matters surfaced in 2026 (a 7-observation inspection at a biologics injectable site, and a warning letter at a formulation plant over an excipient specification) add near-term regulatory uncertainty. On balance this reads as a Hold: a quality franchise that looks fully valued for now, not a name to add to or exit in a hurry.

## Recommendation — Hold

Hold, not Sell, because the underlying franchise shows no structural impairment: India's #4 domestic pharma player growing 14-15% ahead of the market, ROE north of 20%, leverage still modest even after a four-deal acquisition spree, and genuine specialty/biosimilar optionality (Saroglitazar PBC, expanding biosimilar launches, GLP-1 entries). But it is also not a fresh-buy setup at ~21-22x trailing earnings when a meaningful share of that earnings base was cliff-linked (gRevlimid, gMirabegron) and FY27's guided growth leans materially on M&A consolidation rather than pure organic momentum -- corroborated by sell-side consensus targets already sitting below the current price. Two live, plant-specific USFDA risk flags (7-observation Unit 9 GMP-surveillance inspection pending EIR classification; Baddi warning letter) argue against adding exposure until resolved, but neither has escalated to an OAI/import-alert-level threat that would force a Sell call today. Net: hold existing exposure, do not chase the name here; revisit on the Unit-9 EIR classification or the 27-Nov-2026 Saroglitazar PDUFA date.

## Bull case

India formulations -- the #4 domestic pharma franchise -- grew 14-15% YoY in Q4FY26, ahead of the broader market, with a chronic-therapy portfolio (cardiology, respiratory, dermatology) now 46.3% of India sales and management guiding 200-400bps of market outperformance into FY27. The biosimilar and specialty pipeline is genuinely expanding: new India launches (Tishtha/nivolumab biosimilar, Anyra/aflibercept biosimilar, semaglutide GLP-1 brands), a broad biosimilar portfolio, and in the US a real, non-cliff Sitagliptin franchise (Zituvio/Zituvimet/Zituvimet XR) with CVS Caremark formulary access since Jan-2025. Saroglitazar has USFDA priority review for Primary Biliary Cholangitis with a PDUFA date of 27-Nov-2026 -- a credible new US specialty catalyst -- and a licensed generic Palbociclib with potential 180-day exclusivity is guided for a Q4FY27 launch. Returns remain strong (ROE ~20-21%, ROCE ~21%) with leverage still modest even after four acquisitions in a year. As a generics-classified exporter, Zydus's US business is also explicitly exempt (at this time) from the new Section-232 100% tariff on patented-pharmaceutical imports effective 31-Jul-2026, a relative-safety feature versus innovator-exposed peers.

## Bear case

A large share of FY25-FY26's reported profit growth was one-off and cliff-linked: generic Revlimid quota shipments and generic Mirabegron at-risk-launch economics, which management itself has called 'disproportionately high' contributors, are both now normalizing towards zero -- mirabegron capped by the September-2027 licensed-sale deadline agreed after Zydus stipulated Astellas's patents valid and was enjoined from unlicensed sale. FY27's guided 'high teens' revenue growth leans materially on newly consolidated acquisitions (Amplitude Surgical, Agenus, Assertio, Comfort Click) rather than organic momentum, and management's own language puts core North America organic growth at 'single-digit... on a large base.' The balance sheet flipped from net-cash to roughly Rs 3,000 crore net debt funding this acquisition spree. On the regulatory side -- the exact tail risk this desk exists to flag -- a GMP surveillance inspection at the Biologics Unit 9 injectable site (the same broader SEZ complex that carried an OAI as recently as Jun-2024) closed with 7 observations in May-2026, with the EIR classification not yet public, and a separate Warning Letter landed on the Baddi formulation plant in June-2026 over a purified-talc USP-compliance gap. At ~21-22x trailing earnings near a 52-week high, with average sell-side price targets already sitting below the current quote, the stock offers little visible margin of safety against either an adverse Unit-9 classification or a slower-than-guided organic ramp once the one-off contributors fully roll off.

## Bull case

Operating momentum is genuinely strong and broad-based: FY26 sales grew 20.7% YoY to Rs 11,746 crore and Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin expanded to 12.21% (from 11.61% YoY) even as new-entrant headlines swirled, evidence the competitive threat has not yet bitten into realized margins. The mix shift toward distribution (56% of Q4 sales vs 51% YoY) is the more profitable, stickier channel and directly addresses the retail-vs-institutional concern for this sector. The Sanand greenfield plant (78 acres, KEI's largest) is a credible multi-year growth funnel -- LT/HT commercial production already started, with MV and EHV lines due mid-FY27 and Mar-27 -- and management's 17-18% FY27 volume-growth guidance is capacity-backed, not aspirational. The balance sheet is clean (near-debt-free per screener.in, promoter holding ~35% with zero pledge), meaning the Rs 600-700 crore/year capex program is being funded without leverage stress, and the order book carries a genuine export component (Rs 497 crore of Rs 3,585 crore total) that diversifies demand beyond the domestic cycle.

## Bear case

Valuation is stretched on every conventional metric -- trailing P/E of 51-54x, P/B of 7.07x, near multi-year highs -- and the sell-side is split rather than uniformly supportive: Kotak Institutional explicitly rates KEI (alongside Polycab and RR Kabel) Sell on 'full' valuation (22-Jun-2026), Morgan Stanley cut to Equal-weight post-Q4, and even bulls (JPMorgan) trimmed their target. The UltraTech Cement / Adani Kutch Copper entry into wires & cables is a structural threat, not sentiment noise -- both entrants have genuine cost advantages (captive copper, pan-India distribution) that could compress the industry's currently-healthy 11-12% EBITDA margins once they build out distribution, and the initial market reaction (KEI -12.6%) shows the market is already pricing real tail risk here even if the near-term hit has been smaller than feared. EPC (ex-cable) revenue growing 72% YoY in Q4, faster than the core cable business, is a mix-quality watch item since EPC is typically lower-margin and more working-capital intensive. Execution risk on the Sanand MV/EHV ramp (mid-FY27/Mar-27) is un-derisked -- any slippage directly pressures the 17-18% FY27 volume guidance that the current rich multiple is underwriting, and Q1 FY27 results (which would be the first real read on Sanand-driven growth plus early competitive impact) had no confirmed announcement date as of this research. Exports -- the stated next leg of growth -- actually DECLINED ~10% YoY in Q4 FY26 on a Middle East shipping disruption in March 2026 (resumed in April via the Fujairah port at higher freight cost) [DATA - PL Capital Q4FY26 note], a real near-term data point cutting against the FY27E export-mix-expansion thesis (~20% of revenue targeted vs ~15% in FY26).

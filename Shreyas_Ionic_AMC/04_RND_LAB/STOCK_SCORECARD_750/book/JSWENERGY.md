# JSWENERGY

> [!summary]
> JSW Energy posted a strong FY26 on paper (EBITDA +81%, PAT +39%) and has a credible long-dated growth story (13.45GW to a 30GW-by-2030 target, with hydro storage and thermal assets added along the way), but a large chunk of that growth came from buying existing plants (KSK Mahanadi, O2 Power) rather than organic expansion, and the balance sheet has gotten notably more leveraged funding it (net debt roughly 6x EBITDA), forcing the company to raise fresh equity this year. At ~42-50x trailing earnings against an ~8% return on equity, the stock is priced for flawless execution of that growth plan, with little room for a leverage, merchant-price, or receivables surprise -- we rate it a Sell.

## Bull case

Genuine scale story: 13.45GW installed today with credible line-of-sight to 30GW by FY30 via a diversified renewable + thermal + storage (pumped-hydro/BESS) portfolio, backed by JSW Group balance-sheet access (a Rs 4,000cr QIP was placed within days of board approval). FY26 delivered record EBITDA (Rs 11,041cr, +81% YoY) with Q4 EBITDA margin near 50%. The thermal fleet is running at a healthy 73-78% PLF, monetizing the KSK Mahanadi acquisition, and the 26.4GWh pumped-hydro + 3.2GWh BESS locked-in storage platform is a scale advantage few Indian power peers can currently match.

## Bear case

The bear case is capital structure, not growth. Net debt/EBITDA near 6x and interest coverage near 1.7x (quant bs_flag=RED) leave thin room for a merchant-price or execution slip while roughly Rs 20,000cr/year of capex remains to be spent through FY30 -- funded by more debt AND further equity dilution (Rs 10,000cr programme board-approved, Rs 4,000cr already drawn in May-2026). Reported growth is heavily consolidation-driven (KSK Mahanadi + O2 Power), so the >55% revenue-growth headline is not organically repeatable once these assets anniversarize; an ~8% ROE does not support the ~42-50x multiple being paid, and the market has already shown it will punish any miss (Q3 FY26 stock -9% on a sequential EBITDA miss; Q4 FY26 stock -8% despite a headline beat) -- this is a stock priced for near-flawless execution with no valuation cushion, plus an unresolved Maharashtra discom (MSEDCL/APTEL) billing dispute sitting on receivables quality.

## Detailed rationale

JSW Energy is a JSW Group integrated power-generation company with 13.45 GW installed capacity as of Mar-2026 (58% renewable / 42% thermal), spanning hydro, wind, solar, thermal (including the acquired KSK Mahanadi plant, won over Adani Power for Rs 15,985cr) and an emerging pumped-hydro + battery storage platform (29.6 GWh locked-in: 26.4 GWh pumped hydro + 3.2 GWh BESS). FY26 consolidated revenue rose ~57-61% YoY to roughly Rs 18,900-19,900cr and EBITDA rose 81% YoY to a record Rs 11,041cr, with consolidated PAT (pre-minority) of Rs 2,762cr (+39% YoY); Q4 FY26 PAT was Rs 574cr (+38% YoY) at a ~50% EBITDA margin. Earnings-quality flag: the headline growth is substantially inorganic/consolidation-driven. KSK Mahanadi (thermal) and O2 Power (4.7GW renewables, Rs 6,800cr, completed FY26) were both consolidated during the year, which is why the quant model's 1-year revenue growth (~61%) sits at nearly 3x the trailing 3-year CAGR (~22%) -- the quant growth_divergence_flag fires True on exactly this pattern. The quarterly trend underneath the annual headline is choppy: Q3 FY26 (Dec-2025) consolidated PAT was Rs 420cr (+150% YoY off a depressed prior-year base) but operating profit fell ~32% QoQ and missed consensus -- the stock fell ~9% on the print -- because interest cost (Rs 1,417.56cr in the quarter, more than doubled YoY) ate into the EBITDA gain from the larger asset base. Net debt has risen to roughly Rs 65,834cr (Net Debt/EBITDA ~5.96x, Net D/E ~2.14x per press figures; quant shows gross D/E 2.50x and interest coverage of only 1.73x, driving the quant bs_flag=RED). Management partly addressed this via a Rs 4,000cr QIP in May-2026 (7.61cr new shares at Rs 525) under a board-approved Rs 10,000cr raise programme -- i.e. the growth plan is being funded by fresh equity dilution as well as debt, not by free cash flow.

Growth pipeline and residual risk: Strategy 3.0 targets 30GW by FY30 from 13.45GW today (~22% capacity CAGR), with FY27 guidance of ~3GW additions (35-40% wind, rest solar) and ~Rs 20,000cr of capex for the year; a further ~4.6GW pipeline has secured LOIs, and pumped-storage projects (36-month build) already have forest-stage-1 clearance and EPC contracts placed. Against this sits an unresolved Maharashtra discom (MSEDCL) billing dispute -- MSEDCL has appealed to APTEL against a Dec-2025 MERC order that had gone in JSW Energy's favour -- a live receivables/regulatory overhang. At a trailing consolidated P/E of ~42x (quant pe_for_ranking) to ~46-53x (on owners'-profit EPS, per outside estimates) against an ROE of only ~8.1% (quant), the stock is priced well above both typical regulated/IPP power-sector multiples (10-20x) and its own return profile; third-party fair-value estimates cluster near ~29x. Q1 FY27 results are due 2026-07-22 (tomorrow -- not yet reported as of this write-up; no forward number is assumed here, PIT-clean).

# DIVISLAB — Divi's Laboratories Ltd.

> [!summary]
> Divi's Laboratories is a debt-free, best-in-class-on-regulatory-compliance Indian API and custom-synthesis (CDMO) manufacturer that delivered 13% revenue growth and 17% profit growth in FY26, with margins expanding through the year as pricing pressure from Chinese API competitors was offset by a shift toward higher-margin contract manufacturing. Its two main plants have clean recent USFDA inspection records and there is no live regulatory red flag. The concern is valuation: at roughly 75 times trailing earnings, the stock is priced for a much faster growth story (built around GLP-1-adjacent and other custom-synthesis contracts) than the company has yet confirmed - management describes that pipeline as 'strong interest,' not signed business, with commercialization still 12-14 months away. We rate it Hold: a quality, low-risk franchise, but not attractively priced at current levels, with the next results (expected early August 2026) the key near-term check on whether growth is accelerating enough to justify the multiple.

## Recommendation — Hold

Hold, not Sell. The balance-sheet, regulatory and margin-mix fundamentals are genuinely strong and improving - this is not a broken business. FY26's 13% revenue growth, 17% PAT growth and margin expansion (30%->34% OPM through the year) are delivered, reported performance, not promises, and the FDA compliance record (zero 483s at the flagship unit in the latest inspection, one minor observation elsewhere, no live warning letter or import alert) is close to best-in-class for this sector. I am not calling an outright Sell because (a) there is no balance-sheet or regulatory red flag forcing an exit, (b) the delivered numbers are moving in the right direction, and (c) the one large new supply commitment is customer-funded, not purely speculative. But I am also not endorsing the current price: at ~75x trailing earnings and 11.5x book, the market is pricing in a CDMO/GLP-1 growth conversion (Citi's ~25%+ CAGR bull case) that management itself describes as still pending regulatory/customer validation, 12-14 months from commercialization, with no named contract yet disclosed. That is a valuation built on optionality, not on the growth rate actually being delivered (11-13% revenue, high-teens margin-aided PAT). This is a 'don't chase, don't panic-sell' name - Hold, with the Q1 FY27 print (expected ~early Aug-2026) and any Unit-III USFDA inspection outcome as the two nearest checkable catalysts that could tip this toward Sell (if growth disappoints again against a rich multiple) or reinforce Hold (if CDMO/peptide revenue starts to show through).

## Bull case

Divi's is debt-free with interest coverage effectively immaterial given near-zero borrowings, and carries one of the cleanest recent USFDA compliance records among Indian API majors - zero Form-483 observations at its flagship Unit-I in the November 2025 inspection, only one minor procedural observation at the giant Unit-II facility in mid-2024, and no live warning letter or import alert on any site (the sole warning letter was in 2017 and has long since been resolved). Margin expansion through FY26 (30% to 33-34% operating margin) is real and delivered, driven by backward integration and a genuine mix-shift toward custom synthesis. The company has funded, committed capacity growth - the new Kakinada Unit-III site and a customer-funded Rs 650-750cr advanced-intermediates supply agreement signed in May-2025 - rather than purely speculative expansion, and it sits in a credible structural China+1 sourcing tailwind that global innovators are actively using it for, evidenced by management's own description of 'strong multinational interest' in its new peptide/GLP-1-adjacent synthesis capacity.

## Bear case

The strongest bear point is that valuation has run well ahead of demonstrated growth. At roughly 75x trailing earnings and 11.5x book for a business compounding revenue at 11-13% (3-year CAGR 10.78%, FY26 13%) and profit at high-teens largely on margin/mix rather than volume, the multiple is pricing the bull-case CDMO/GLP-1 ramp as a near-certainty when management's own language is still 'strong interest,' '12-14 months to commercialization,' and explicitly not generic-GLP-1 volumes. Sell-side commentary itself flags that the CDMO ramp has already been slower than initially projected in parts of the pipeline, and Chinese-competitor API pricing pressure (cited at 15-20% on select molecules) has been a genuine, multi-quarter drag rather than a one-off. If the CDMO/peptide conversion slips further, or if generic-API pricing pressure persists longer than the market currently assumes, the stock has meaningful room to de-rate on multiple compression alone, independent of any operating shortfall. Add to this that Unit-III (Kakinada) has no reported USFDA inspection yet - a clean record elsewhere does not guarantee a clean first inspection at a newly commissioned, larger site once it scales into commercial CDMO/API batches.

## Valuation (reverse-DCF judgment)

At ~75x trailing EPS and ~11.5x book, the market is effectively underwriting sustained 20-25%+ earnings compounding for years (consistent with Citi's bull case of roughly 3x revenue and 4x EBITDA over FY25-FY30). The business as actually reported is compounding revenue at 11-13% and profit at high-teens (margin-driven, not volume-driven) - roughly half the implied rate. To organically clear this multiple, Divi's needs its peptide/GLP-1-adjacent CDMO pipeline and the new advanced-intermediates supply agreement to convert from pipeline interest into booked, ramping revenue within the next 4-6 quarters - exactly the window management itself has flagged (12-14 months to commercialization). Until that shows up in a print, the multiple is pricing hope rather than delivered growth. Judgment: rich, not fair, at current levels - a quality business, priced for outcomes it has not yet proven.

## Detailed rationale

Divi's Laboratories is India's largest pure-play API/CDMO manufacturer, split between generic-API sales and custom synthesis (CDMO) work for global innovator pharma companies, plus a smaller, fast-growing nutraceuticals business (as of the Jun-2025/Q1 FY26 print: generics ~47%, custom synthesis ~53%, nutraceuticals ~Rs 250cr and +40% YoY; exports ~88-89% of revenue with Europe the largest market and the US ~29%).

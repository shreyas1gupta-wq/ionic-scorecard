## Detailed rationale

Alembic Pharmaceuticals is a Vadodara-headquartered generics/API manufacturer with a large India-branded formulations franchise and an emerging US-branded specialty platform (Pivya, via the Utility Therapeutics acquisition) layered on its core US generics, ex-US generics and API businesses. FY26 (ended Mar-2026) consolidated revenue grew 10% YoY to Rs 7,344.9 cr with reported net profit up to Rs 674.77 cr [DATA - Business Standard/scanx, 15-May-2026]. Q4 FY26 segment mix: India branded Rs 568 cr (+4%), US formulations Rs 564 cr (+11%, six new launches), ex-US generics Rs 369 cr, API Rs 347 cr (+2%) [DATA].

Earnings-quality flag (per skill's one-off hunt): the last two reported quarters both carried distorting one-offs. Net effect: FY26's headline profit growth overstates the underlying run-rate, which normalized looks closer to flat-to-modest. This is already widely flagged by sell-side commentary the same week, so I do not treat it as an escalation-worthy structural gap the quant model can't see - it is ordinary (if unusually clustered) quarterly noise.

Regulatory history is a recurring-but-non-escalating pattern typical of a multi-site generics manufacturer: Form-483s at Jarod (Nov-2024, 5 obs), the Vadodara Bioequivalence facility (Mar-2025, 1 procedural obs), Karkhadi (Mar-2023, 2 obs), Panelav (Oct-2022, 4 obs; API I&II re-inspected May-2025) - none has escalated to an OAI/warning-letter/import-alert against an Alembic-owned manufacturing site to date [DATA]. The one live 2026 item - a USFDA Warning Letter dated 10-Jul-2026 - was issued to the third-party Clinical Investigator on a bioequivalence study at the Vadodara BE facility over an Informed Consent Form issue, not to Alembic itself; the company states no data-integrity impact and no restriction on facility operations [DATA - Tradebrains/Medical Dialogues, Jul-2026]. Strategically, management is deliberately skipping the first wave of global semaglutide generic launches (targeting a later India-first pen launch), while building the US-branded Pivya platform and guiding a 25-30 product US pipeline (complex generics/injectables) at mid-teens constant-currency growth for FY27-28 [DATA - BusinessToday/IndiaMedToday, Nov-2025/2026].

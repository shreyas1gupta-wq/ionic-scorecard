# TORNTPHARM — Torrent Pharmaceuticals Ltd.

> [!summary]
> Torrent Pharmaceuticals just completed India's biggest recent pharma buyout, folding in JB Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals to build a much larger company with added international and contract-manufacturing capability. The underlying India business is genuinely strong, growing faster than the industry on its chronic-disease drug portfolio, and the company has a credible three-year plan to pay down the debt taken on for the deal. But the stock trades near its all-time high at roughly 74-77 times trailing earnings - a rich price even for a high-quality business - and most of the earnings growth priced in over the next two years comes from folding in a full year of the acquired company's numbers rather than the underlying business simply growing faster. With debt elevated, the acquisition still in its early integration innings, one plant's regulatory status not fully confirmed as clean, and ongoing patent litigation around its new diabetes-drug franchise, we do not see enough cushion in the price to recommend holding it here - our call is Sell.

## Recommendation — Sell

Sell over Hold because valuation is the dominant, quantifiable factor and it is stretched by any reasonable yardstick - the current multiple already prices in years of flawless synergy delivery, continued mid-teens-plus organic growth, and no negative surprise, on a franchise that has just taken on its largest-ever debt-funded acquisition with only one quarter of consolidated numbers reported so far. Even sell-side's own optimistic FY28 EPS build does not get the forward multiple into cheap territory, and the two brokerage targets cited sit at (Rs 4,800) or below (Rs 4,400) CMP. Against this sits a genuinely excellent business (27%+ ROE, chronic-led India moat outgrowing IPM, a credible cost-synergy roadmap) that argues for Hold rather than a harder call - but the balance tips to Sell on valuation discipline plus the unresolved Indrad 483 status, live semaglutide patent litigation, and Germany/Brazil FX drags stacking up as real, live risks with no offsetting margin of safety in the price.

## Bull case

Torrent runs one of the best India-branded-generics franchises in the country - 5th largest in the domestic pharma market, chronic-therapy-heavy (cardiac, CNS, anti-diabetic), and it keeps outgrowing the industry (15% India base growth in the Mar-2026 quarter versus ~10% industry growth), with 27%+ return on equity and 32%+ EBITDA margins few peers match. The JB Chemicals acquisition is strategically sound, not opportunistic: it adds scale, a CDMO/API manufacturing arm Torrent did not have organically, and international branded-generics exposure, and management has laid out a specific deleveraging path (net debt/EBITDA peaking at 2.8x in FY27, targeted below 0.5x within two-to-three years) backed by Rs 450cr of guided run-rate synergies. Its new generic-semaglutide franchise (Semalix/Sembolic) is a genuine multi-year growth lever - Torrent moved early ahead of Novo Nordisk's India patent expiry and already holds an estimated 38% combined share of the fast-growing oral-plus-injectable generic GLP-1 market, a rare organic growth driver layered on top of the steady branded-generics base.

## Bear case

The single biggest problem is price: at ~74-77x trailing earnings and ~39x EV/EBITDA, Torrent trades roughly double its own historical premium band and well above what even sell-side's most optimistic post-merger EPS numbers can justify - Motilal Oswal's own Rs 4,400 target (45x forward earnings) sits below the current share price, and ICICI Securities' Hold-rated target of Rs 4,800 is barely above it, meaning the desks closest to the story are not calling this undervalued either. The company has gone deep into debt (borrowings of Rs 15,026cr, guided net debt/EBITDA peaking near 2.8x in FY27) to fund its largest-ever acquisition, and the deleveraging and Rs 450cr synergy targets are guidance, not yet delivered - only one quarter of consolidated JB Chemicals numbers has been reported, so integration execution risk is real and untested. Layer on: the Indrad plant's most recent (Jun-2024) USFDA Form-483 with five observations has no confirmed resolution as of this research (its predecessor OAI and the older 2019 warning letter were both cleared, but this newer one is an open question, not a clean bill); the Novo Nordisk semaglutide dispute is resolved only by an interim formulation workaround, not a final ruling, with the underlying patent upheld valid to 2031-32; a Jul-2026 voluntary recall of select Semalix injection pens (a Dr Reddy's manufacturing-quality issue) shows the new GLP-1 franchise is not friction-free; and Germany's tender-driven pricing pressure plus Brazilian-real currency swings are structural drags on the international book. ROCE has also come down to 15.2% against a 27.4% ROE - a gap that reflects leverage doing more of the work than pure capital efficiency post-acquisition.

## Valuation (reverse-DCF judgment)

At ~74-77x trailing consolidated earnings and ~39x EV/EBITDA, the market is pricing Torrent for a growth and execution outcome well beyond what the underlying, M&A-effect-stripped business has ever delivered. Organic-like growth across the combined franchise - India base ~15%, Brazil high-teens-to-20s in reported (partly FX-flattered) rupee terms, JB Chemicals domestic +20%/international +4%, Germany flat-to-negative, US small and fast-growing but under 10% of revenue - nets out to a genuine mid-teens (roughly 14-16%) blended organic grower. The FY27-28 EPS ramp brokerages are modeling (Motilal Oswal: Rs 66 to Rs 92, +39% YoY) is overwhelmingly a mechanical artifact of full-year JB Chemicals consolidation, synergy capture, and falling interest costs as debt is repaid - not a step-change in organic demand. Even granting that ramp in full, the stock only falls to ~52x FY28E earnings, still a scarcity premium multiple, and MOFSL's own 45x-forward-earnings-based target (Rs 4,400) sits below today's price. To earn an equity-like return from here, an investor needs the combined entity to sustain mid-teens-plus organic growth for years, deliver the full Rs 450cr synergy program on schedule, delever from 2.8x to under 0.5x net debt/EBITDA without a QIP-driven dilution surprise, and have the market continue to award a 45-75x multiple to a business whose consolidated ROCE has actually fallen to 15.2% post-deal. That is a demanding, low-margin-of-safety ask - verdict: rich, not fair.

## Detailed rationale

Torrent just closed its largest-ever acquisition - the roughly Rs 25,700cr (~$3bn enterprise value) buyout of JB Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals from KKR-backed Tau Investment Holdings, structured as a 46.39% promoter-stake purchase (~Rs 11,917cr) plus ~2.8% from JBCP employees (~Rs 719cr) plus a mandatory SEBI open offer for another 26% (~Rs 6,842.8cr). Torrent reached 48.80% ownership by 3-Feb-2026, and NCLT sanctioned the amalgamation of the remaining minority shareholders (51 Torrent shares per 100 JBCP shares) effective 8-Jul-2026. JB Chemicals' own FY25 (last full pre-deal year) was a decent asset - revenue Rs 3,918cr (+12.4%), PAT Rs 659.5cr (+19.3%), EBITDA Rs 1,087cr - but Torrent paid a rich ~24.8x EV/EBITDA / ~38.9x EV/PAT on FY25 numbers for control, funded almost entirely with debt: Rs 12,500-14,000cr of bonds (1-5yr maturities, priced 7.15-7.5%, expected AA+ rating) plus commercial paper. Consolidated borrowings already stand at Rs 15,026cr as of Mar-2026; management guides gross net-debt/EBITDA to peak at 2.8x in FY27 before falling below 0.5x within two-to-three years, aided by Rs 450cr of guided run-rate cost synergies by year three (~20% front-loaded in year one). A Rs 5,000cr enabling QIP approval also sits on the books - not drawn yet, but a live dilution overhang if deleveraging slips.

Operating performance: FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 13,980cr (+21% YoY) and PAT Rs 2,138cr (+12% YoY vs FY25's Rs 1,911cr) - profit growth lagged revenue growth because Q4FY26 alone carried Rs 66cr of acquisition-related exceptional items (Rs 46cr Torrent-side, Rs 19cr JB-side), and the deal's debt is already running through the P&L before the full synergy offset shows up. Q4FY26 in isolation: revenue Rs 4,197cr (+42%, inflated by JB Chemicals' first full quarter of consolidation from January 2026), EBITDA Rs 1,356cr (+41%, margin 32.3% consolidated / 32.7% base business), PAT down 27% YoY to Rs 364cr. The underlying India franchise is genuinely strong: base business (ex-JB) grew 15% in Q4FY26 versus IPM growth of ~10% - Torrent is the 5th-largest player in the Indian Pharma Market, chronic-heavy (cardiac, CNS, anti-diabetic), with Curatio's OTC dermatology arm up 27% in FY26. Brazil revenue was Rs 455cr (+30%, partly currency-flattered) and the US book, still small at Rs 396cr (~9-10% of consolidated revenue), grew 31% on ~10 planned FY26 launches including esomeprazole and, the standout, generic oral/injectable semaglutide (Semalix/Sembolic), where Torrent already holds an estimated 38% combined market share after an early launch ahead of Novo Nordisk's India patent expiry. That launch is not litigation-free: Novo sued on a separate formulation patent (IN 325669, upheld valid to 2031-32 by a Delhi HC ruling), and on 20-Mar-2026 the Delhi HC recorded an interim arrangement requiring Torrent to keep its SNAC concentration (0.53mmol) outside Novo's claimed 0.6-2.1mmol range - a workaround, not a final resolution. Separately, on 9-Jul-2026 Torrent voluntarily recalled select 2mg/4mg Semalix injection pen batches made by contract partner Dr Reddy's after a quality notification (tablets, 8mg pens, and other-manufacturer pens unaffected; no patient-safety issue claimed) - a domestic supply-chain event, not a USFDA action, but a reminder that scaling this franchise fast carries execution risk. Germany remains a small (~Rs 300cr/quarter), structurally low-growth drag under the Krankenkassen tender system. Q1 FY27 (Jun-2026 quarter) results are NOT yet out as of this research - board meets 30-Jul-2026; consensus revenue estimates sit at Rs 4,094-4,611cr versus Q1FY26's Rs 3,178cr revenue / Rs 548cr PAT.

Regulatory and valuation picture: recent USFDA trend is clean - Torrent's Dahej plant passed with zero observations (Jan-2026), Bileshwarpura oncology unit passed with zero observations (Apr-2026), and JB Chemicals' Panoli API and T20 formulations plants both cleared USFDA with zero observations (Mar-2025). The one open thread is Indrad (Taluka-Kadi, Gujarat): its 2019 warning letter was formally closed out by FDA in Sep-2024, and a separate Sep-2022 OAI classification was resolved to VAI via an EIR around Aug-2024 - but a newer Jun-2024 inspection (PAI+GMP) produced a fresh Form 483 with five observations, and this research could not confirm that 483's final disposition (closeout, VAI, or escalation) as of Jul-2026 - flagged as a genuine data gap, not assumed clean. On valuation: at a share price near all-time highs (~Rs 4,700-4,950) and ~Rs 1.61 lakh crore market cap, Torrent trades at roughly 74-77x trailing consolidated P/E, ~39x EV/EBITDA, ~20x P/B, with ROE 27.4% but ROCE only 15.2% - a gap that says leverage, not just capital efficiency, is doing a lot of the post-deal work. Even sell-side's own bullish combined-entity EPS build (Motilal Oswal: Rs 66 FY27E, Rs 92 FY28E, +39% YoY driven mostly by full-year JB consolidation, synergy capture, and falling interest cost rather than organic demand) only brings the multiple down to ~52x on FY28E - and MOFSL's own target of Rs 4,400 (45x forward earnings) sits below CMP; ICICI Securities' Hold-rated Rs 4,800 target is roughly in line with CMP. Street consensus is already clustered at or below today's price.

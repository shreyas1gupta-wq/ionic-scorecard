## Bull case

360 ONE WAM is the dominant listed pure-play in India's structurally under-penetrated UHNI/HNI wealth and alternatives (AIF) market, with AUM compounding to a record ₹7.77 lakh crore and quarterly ARR net inflows of ₹10,815cr showing the franchise is still winning new money, not just riding market beta. The Asset Management segment is the standout, growing revenue 46.7% YoY in the latest quarter and diversifying the earnings base away from pure wealth-distribution economics. ROE of 14.4% is respectable for an asset-light model, the company remains a consistent dividend payer (₹6/share interim), and Bain Capital's continued anchor presence alongside founder-CEO Karan Bhagat provides institutional-quality governance oversight for a business where regulatory and fiduciary conduct is the central risk.

## Bear case

Profit growth has decelerated and is now trailing revenue growth in back-to-back quarters - Q4 FY26 PAT fell 11.8% YoY despite 36% revenue growth, and Q1 FY27 PAT grew only 16.1% against 26.8% revenue growth - with the core Wealth Management segment's PBT actually falling 6.1% YoY even as its revenue rose 25.4%, indicating real fee/margin compression (ARR yields down ~5bps QoQ), not a clean one-off. A large share of AUM growth is now transactional/brokerage AUM that moves with market levels, so a market drawdown would hit reported AUM and revenue harder than the headline growth suggests. The stock's 36x P/E and 4.7x P/B already price in sustained 20%+ compounding that the last two quarters have not delivered. Separately, a January-2025 Income-Tax Department search on the IIFL Finance/360 ONE group culminated in an April-2026 assessment order demanding ₹336.14cr (₹192.42cr on 360 ONE WAM itself) for FY18-FY25 - contested and provisioned as a dispute, but a live legal overhang and a reminder that promoter-linked group entities remain under tax-authority scrutiny.

Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026) is OUT - released 16-Jul-2026: revenue from ops ₹1,226cr (+26.8% YoY from ₹966.58cr), net profit ₹330.53cr (+16.1% YoY) - profit growth trailing revenue growth again. AUM stood at ₹7.77 lakh crore (ARR AUM ₹3.42L cr, Transactional/Brokerage AUM ₹4.35L cr), with ARR net inflows of ₹10,815cr for the quarter. Segment detail is the key earnings-quality tell: Wealth Management revenue grew 25.4% to ₹967.34cr but its PBT FELL 6.1% YoY to ₹237.95cr - core-segment margin compression, not a one-off - while Asset Management revenue grew a much faster 46.7% to ₹305.34cr [DATA - Business Standard/AlphaStreet, 16-Jul-2026].

## Detailed rationale

Coromandel International (Murugappa Group) is India's #2 phosphatic-fertiliser player and largest private-sector NPK/complex-fertiliser maker, with an expanding crop-protection arm built via its controlling 53.73% stake in NACL Industries (Rs 820 Cr deal, FY26), targeting a Rs 10,000 Cr crop-protection revenue base long-term [DATA - coromandel.biz, agribusinessglobal.com]. FY26 (year to Mar-2026): consolidated total income Rs 31,827 Cr (+30% YoY, per indianchemicalnews.com; whalesbook.com cites Rs 30,882 Cr/+26% - sources agree on high-20s% growth), EBITDA Rs 3,232 Cr (+23% YoY), but EBITDA margin compressed to ~10% from ~12% in FY25 on higher phosphoric-acid/sulphur input costs and subsidy-timing/inventory effects [DATA - whalesbook.com]. Reported PAT fell 8% YoY to Rs 1,898 Cr - but this is largely a base-effect optical: Q4FY25 carried a non-recurring Rs 347 Cr land-sale gain, while Q4FY26 carries a Rs 70.56 Cr consolidated impairment on the Drone subsidiary (order-execution delays) [DATA - Innovacia Insights, tradebrains.in, marketsmojo.com]. Strip both one-offs and Q4 EBITDA still grew 16% YoY; the real (non-one-off) deterioration is PAT margin slipping to 6.5% from 8%, driven by the raw-material cost cycle. Debt rose to Rs 1,506 Cr (FY26) from Rs 780 Cr (FY25) funding the Rs 1,100 Cr Kakinada backward-integration project (2,000 TPD sulphuric acid + 650 TPD phosphoric acid, commissioned Q4FY26) plus the NACL acquisition. Subsidy remains ~85% of FY26 revenue and ~66% of FY26 EBITDA; outstanding subsidy receivable was Rs 2,168 Cr as of Mar-2026 against Rs 10,649 Cr received in the year - a structural, sector-wide working-capital/timing risk, not company-specific mismanagement. ROE 16.4%, ROCE 22.0% remain healthy [DATA - screener.in]. Q1 FY27 results are NOT yet out; board meets 23-Jul-2026 to approve them [DATA - marketscreener.com financial calendar]. At CMP ~Rs 2,045, P/E is 31.1x, P/B 4.8x - rich against a 3-year profit CAGR of -1% and a 5-year profit CAGR of only 8% (versus 17% 5-year sales CAGR, i.e., margins have been compressing for a multi-year stretch, not just this quarter) [DATA - screener.in].

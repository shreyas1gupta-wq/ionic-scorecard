## Bull case

The 1P inventory-model shift is a completed structural change, not a plan - ~90% of NOV already runs through owned inventory by Q3FY26, contributing over half of a ~1 percentage-point consolidated EBITDA-margin gain, and it was unlocked by a real regulatory milestone (the board's April 2025 cap on foreign ownership at 49.5% to secure Indian-Owned-and-Controlled-Company status under FEMA, approved by over 99% of shareholders). ## Bear case

Consolidated profitability is still genuinely thin, not just optically so because of trailing-PE mechanics - FY26 full-year PAT margin was just 0.67% (Rs 366 cr on Rs 54,364 cr revenue) and Q4FY26 consolidated adjusted EBITDA margin was only ~2.4% of adjusted revenue, so the quant screen's near-zero ROE/ROCE is a fair description of where the business actually is today, not solely a stale-data artifact. The headline revenue growth numbers (168.6% 1-year, 97.3% 3-year CAGR per the quant snapshot; 196% YoY in Q4) meaningfully overstate real demand growth because Blinkit's 1P inventory shift moved gross sale value onto the P&L instead of commission-only revenue - the true volume metric (Blinkit NOV) grew a still-strong-but-much-more-moderate 95.4% YoY, and this recognition-driven inflation needs to be understood before extrapolating the headline growth rate forward. At an estimated EV/run-rate-NOV of ~2.6-2.7x, the market is pricing sustained rapid growth plus material margin expansion toward a Meituan-like mature-market destination, and Meituan itself is the cautionary tale: its historically superior ~25% take-rate/4%-of-GMV profitability was overwhelmed in 2025 by a horizontal-platform subsidy war (JD.com and Alibaba), pushing it to an expected RMB 24 billion net loss - a structurally identical risk sits in India with Amazon Now and Flipkart Minutes each scaling past 500 dark stores (Flipkart adding ~100/month) and a contribution-positive JioMart already at ~800 stores. Blinkit's own store-count ramp (2,243 at Q4FY26-end) needs to accelerate materially, not just continue, to hit management's 3,000-store target by March 2027, and a live, recurring ESOP dilution stream (Rs 167 cr in fresh grants in April 2026 alone; ~Rs 419 cr realized by insiders) is a real per-share drag that headline adjusted-EBITDA figures do not capture and that I could not fully quantify from public disclosures.

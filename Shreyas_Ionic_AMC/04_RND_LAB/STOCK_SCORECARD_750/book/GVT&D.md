## Detailed rationale

GE Vernova T&D India makes high-voltage/extra-high-voltage transmission equipment (power transformers, gas-insulated switchgear, circuit breakers, digital grid/protection systems) and is the Indian listed arm of GE Vernova's global Grid Solutions business. It sits at the centre of India's grid capex supercycle: PGCIL/state transmission capex, renewable-energy evacuation infrastructure, discom modernization, and a prospective HVDC (high-voltage DC) build-out. FY26 (year ended Mar-2026) revenue was Rs 6,210cr, EBITDA Rs 1,680cr at a 27.1% margin, and PAT Rs 1,230cr, up 103% YoY. Q4 FY26 alone: revenue Rs 1,640cr (+42% YoY), EBITDA margin 27.2%, PAT Rs 352cr (+89% YoY). Order inflow for FY26 was Rs 14,776cr (+37% YoY), leaving an outstanding order book of ~Rs 21,456cr as of Mar-2026 (private sector 76% of backlog) - a book-to-bill of roughly 3.5x trailing revenue, which is genuinely strong multi-year visibility for a capital-goods name. Balance sheet is close to debt-free (D/E ~0.01, interest coverage >100x), ROE ~23%, ROCE ~68%.

The critical earnings-quality point: PAT growth (103% FY26, 89% Q4) ran far ahead of revenue growth (30-44%), driven almost entirely by EBITDA margin expansion to 27%+ from a materially lower base a few years back - a mix of operating leverage, a global shortage of high-voltage equipment capacity supporting pricing, and favourable product/export mix, not one-off items (no asset-sale gains, DTA credits, or merger base-effects were found in the available disclosures). Management itself guides only to '20%+ OPM' as the sustainable margin, i.e. it does not fully endorse FY26's 27% print as the new run-rate; sell-side (PL Capital) downgraded the stock to Accumulate post-Q4 specifically on valuation even while calling growth momentum intact. For FY27, management points to Rs 7,000-8,000cr of base domestic orders (ex-HVDC, +10-15% YoY) plus ~Rs 2,500cr of export orders aided by GE Vernova's 'Asia for Asia' localization strategy, with a national HVDC pipeline of ~Rs 1 lakh crore+ expected to be AWARDED (not yet booked) over the next 2-3 years. That HVDC number is TAM/opportunity, not backlog - the order-book-forensics distinction (announced vs executable vs won) that matters most here, since the ~95x trailing PE is pricing a large chunk of that pipeline landing on GVT&D specifically and on a compressed timeline, against multiple credible competitors (Hitachi Energy, Siemens Energy, BHEL's HVDC push) chasing the same tenders.

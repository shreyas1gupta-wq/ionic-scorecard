## Bull case

Biosimilars (~60% of revenue) grew 16% in FY26 with EBITDA margin expanding ~200bps to 22%, and the Viatris stake buyout (closed Q1 CY2026) removes the last minority/governance overhang, giving Biocon full economic and operational control of Biocon Biologics. The Malaysia insulin plant - the single biggest historical regulatory drag (OAI since 2023) - is now FDA-VAI and freshly EMA-approved for a new production line, unlocking EU insulin supply from Q2FY27 and adding capacity just as Biocon pushes into GLP-1-adjacent complex generics (US FDA-approved generic liraglutide, Mar-2026) and semaglutide biosimilar filings in Canada/Brazil. Debt refinancing has pushed the bulk of Biocon Biologics' ~$1.35bn acquisition-debt maturities out to FY28/29, buying runway for EBITDA to catch up to the leverage load. Sector-wide, the Section 232 100% pharma tariff (effective 31-Jul-2026) explicitly excludes generics/biosimilars 'at this time' - Biocon's US book is almost entirely generics/biosimilars, so near-term tariff exposure is low.

## Bear case

The headline P/E of ~183-184x (vs a healthcare-sector median of ~40x) is real even after adjusting for one-offs: net margin is structurally thin (~2.2-2.6%) because interest and amortization on the Biologics acquisition debt absorb most of the EBITDA improvement, and management itself is guiding to a slower 'consolidation phase' for FY27 rather than further margin step-change - the market may be extrapolating a growth/margin trajectory the company isn't promising for next year. CRDMO/research-services growth is weak (+2-3% YoY) and a drag on the blended growth story. A fresh USFDA Form 483 (5 observations) was issued at the Bengaluru biosimilars PLI site in Apr-2026; while framed as procedural, any biologics-site 483 carries a non-trivial historical OAI/warning-letter escalation tail the desk tracks, and CAPA resolution is still open. Semaglutide's India launch has been delayed amid a domestic price war, ceding first-mover ground domestically even as Biocon prioritizes international launches.

# MANKIND

> [!summary]
> Mankind Pharma had a strong FY26 on paper - revenue up 17% and profit up over 30% - powered by its domestic chronic-disease business and the newly integrated Bharat Serums & Vaccines unit. But a meaningful part of that profit jump came from a low tax rate that management says will rise sharply in FY27 (from ~15-16% to ~25-26%), plus some one-off items - so next year's profit growth could look much slower than this year's even if the underlying business keeps growing at a healthy pace. At roughly 55x trailing earnings, the stock is priced for more than that normalized growth path can obviously deliver. We are maintaining Hold: the business is sound and well-capitalized, but the valuation leaves little room for the tax-driven earnings air pocket we expect in FY27.

## Bull case

Domestic branded-generics leadership with a chronic-therapy tilt (structurally stickier, higher-margin than acute) gives Mankind a durable growth runway independent of the US pricing/tariff cycle that dogs export-heavy pharma peers. The BSV acquisition is integrating well and adding a fertility/critical-care growth vector; consumer healthcare (+20% YoY in Q4) is a genuine second engine with strong brand recall (Manforce, Prega News). Balance sheet is clean (debt_equity 0.39, interest coverage 5.6x, bs_flag GREEN, liquidity_flag GREEN), management is guiding double-digit growth into FY27 with margins intact (25.5-26.5% EBITDA band), and the GLP-1 + Vadodara biotech capex are credible option value for the next leg, not yet in numbers.

## Bear case

The valuation (~55x trailing PE, value_score 45.4/100 - below the universe median) is not just pricing in high-teens revenue growth, it is implicitly counting on FY26's unusually low ~15-16% effective tax rate persisting - which management itself says will not happen (guided to 25-26% in FY27). That alone mechanically caps FY27 reported PAT growth well below FY26's, independent of how well the underlying business performs, and the market has not obviously priced that air pocket in. Q4's headline 203% standalone PAT growth was also boosted by Rs 22.8 cr of non-recurring exceptional items. Heavy FY27 capex (6-7% of revenue) toward a multi-year Vadodara biotech build will pressure free cash flow before it contributes revenue, and GLP-1 is still an early-stage bet, not a proven contributor. None of this is a business-quality problem - it is a 'paying up for an earnings number that's about to normalize down' problem.

## Detailed rationale

Mankind Pharma is India's #2 domestic branded-generics player (by prescription volume), ~85% of revenue from the Indian market, built on a large field force across acute + chronic therapies, plus a scaled consumer-healthcare franchise (Manforce, Prega News, Gas-O-Fast, Nimulid). FY26 (year to Mar-2026, consolidated) revenue was Rs 14,278 cr, +17% YoY from Rs 12,207 cr in FY25, with growth increasingly driven by the chronic segment and the integration of Bharat Serums & Vaccines (BSV, fertility/critical-care), acquired in the prior year. Q4 FY26 alone: consolidated revenue Rs 3,443 cr (+11.8% YoY), consolidated PAT ~Rs 554-559 cr (+30.4% YoY, sources vary slightly); standalone revenue Rs 2,581.8 cr (+22% YoY) with standalone PAT surging 203% YoY to Rs 713.6 cr. Segment detail for the quarter: domestic Rs 2,886 cr (+13.4% YoY), exports Rs 557 cr (+4.2% YoY), consumer healthcare +20% YoY. [DATA, multiple financial-news sources, see research_sources]. Q1 FY27 (Jun-2026) has NOT been reported as of this write-up (2026-07-21) - board meeting/earnings call is scheduled for 30-Jul-2026 - so this call rests on FY26 full-year + Q4 FY26 as the latest prints, per instruction.

Earnings-quality flag (material): the Q4/FY26 PAT surge was flattered by two things beyond core operations - (1) Rs 22.8 cr of standalone exceptional items (labour-code and stamp-duty costs tied to an IP-transfer agreement, i.e. non-recurring), and more importantly (2) an unusually low effective tax rate of 15-16% in FY26. Management itself has guided the FY27 effective tax rate up to 25-26%. That is a ~9-10pp mechanical step-up that will compress reported PAT growth in FY27 even if the underlying operating business keeps growing at management's guided high-teens top-line pace and 25.5-26.5% EBITDA margin band. There is also a source discrepancy on full-year FY26 consolidated PAT itself - some reports show ~Rs 1,938 cr, others ~Rs 2,037.56 cr for the same period; I could not reconcile this from the headlines alone and am flagging it rather than picking one silently [DATA - unreconciled, see sources].

Strategically, management is scaling a GLP-1 portfolio aimed at endocrinologists (early-stage, optionality not yet in the base), building out vitamins/minerals/protein-supplement adjacencies, and committing 6-7% of FY27 revenue to capex, largely a phased biotech facility in Vadodara - a multi-year investment cycle that will pressure free cash flow before it adds revenue. No USFDA 483/warning-letter/import-alert action was found in searches specific to Mankind's plants as of 2026-07-21 [DATA - absence of finding, not confirmation of zero risk]; consistent with the business being overwhelmingly domestic-branded rather than US-export-dependent, so the sector's usual USFDA tail-risk framework applies with less force here than to a pure-play exporter.

# FORTIS

> [!summary]
> Fortis had a strong FY26: revenue up 17% and PAT up 32% on genuine hospital-occupancy and pricing gains, with margins improving and a credible plan to add ~500-600 beds and lift EBITDA margin further in FY27. The stock, though, already trades at a rich ~70x earnings and ~39x EBITDA multiple -- well above hospital peers -- so most of that good news looks priced in, and a modest one-time debt-funded acquisition spree has nudged leverage up. We rate it a Hold: a well-run, growing hospital business, but not cheap enough here to add.

## Bull case

Real double-digit revenue growth (FY26 +17.3%) driven by genuine ARPOB and occupancy gains, not financial engineering; EBITDA margin already up ~250bps YoY to 22.8% with a credible, quantified path to 25% by FY28; ~500-600 new beds coming on stream in FY27 extend the organic growth runway; Agilus diagnostics is turning the margin corner off FY26's 8.5% revenue growth; balance-sheet D/E remains low (0.35x) despite two bolt-on hospital acquisitions funded in FY26.

## Bear case

Priced for perfection: P/E ~69-70x and EV/EBITDA ~39x sit well above listed peers (Narayana Hrudayalaya ~27.5x, Global Health ~33x EV/EBITDA), while trailing ROE of just 9.25% shows the earnings the multiple is capitalizing largely aren't here yet -- it is a bet on flawless execution of the bed-ramp and margin guidance. Leverage is rising, not falling: net-debt/EBITDA moved from 0.41x to ~1.24x through FY26 on the Shrimann and People Tree acquisitions, and further M&A-funded growth would add to that. Any occupancy slip (already 68% vs a recent 69% high) or integration hiccup at the newly acquired hospitals would compress a rich multiple faster than earnings can grow into it.

## Valuation (reverse-DCF judgment)

Rich, not cheap. ## Detailed rationale

Fortis Healthcare runs a ~15,000+ bed hospital chain (~27 facilities) plus a ~65% economic stake in Agilus Diagnostics (formerly SRL); hospitals are ~85% of consolidated revenue. FY26 (year ended Mar-2026): consolidated revenue Rs 9,128 Cr (+17.3% YoY), operating EBITDA Rs 2,085 Cr (+31.3%, margin 22.8% vs 20.4% in FY25), PAT Rs 1,064 Cr (+31.5%). Q4 FY26 alone: revenue Rs 2,365 Cr (+17.8%), EBITDA Rs 531 Cr (+22.2%, margin 22.5%), PAT Rs 271 Cr (+44.2%). Hospital segment FY26 revenue Rs 7,773 Cr (+19.1%) on ARPOB gains (+10.2% YoY in the Jun-2025 quarter) and occupancy in the high-60s%; Agilus diagnostics FY26 revenue Rs 1,527 Cr (+8.5%) with margins recovering off a low base. Board declared a Rs 1/share final dividend. Q1 FY27 (Jun-2026) has NOT been reported as of this review (21-Jul-2026); no board-meeting date for it surfaced in sources checked, so this call rests on the FY26/Q4FY26 print plus FY27 guidance.

Earnings quality: FY26 carried a net exceptional LOSS of Rs 22.2 Cr (a one-time Labour Code employee-benefit provision of Rs 55.2 Cr, partly offset by a Rs 33 Cr impairment reversal in an associate) -- immaterial at ~2% of PAT, and if anything modestly UNDERSTATES core growth (i.e. no inflation-via-one-off concern here). More consequential is the debt-funded M&A: Fortis bought Shrimann Superspecialty Hospital (~Rs 462 Cr) and People Tree Hospital (~Rs 430 Cr) in FY26, pushing net debt to ~Rs 2,547 Cr and net-debt/EBITDA from 0.41x to ~1.24x through the year (quant D/E on the full balance sheet is still a low 0.35x, so absolute leverage stays manageable, but the trend is rising, not falling). Management's FY27 guidance: 15%+ organic hospital revenue growth, ~150bps further EBITDA margin uplift, ~500-600 new beds commissioning, diagnostics margin to 23-24%, and a house target of 25% consolidated EBITDA margin by FY28 (~1.5-2.0pp/yr) -- assertive, but the FY26 print already delivered ~250bps of margin gain, so it is not obviously unrealistic.

Valuation / reverse-DCF: quant flags quality_score 69.8/100, ROE only 9.25%, ROCE 14.35%, PE_current ~69.8x, final_score_3y 48.6 and final_score_1y 41.6 -- Hold on both windows, both High coverage confidence. Independent broker data corroborates a rich tape: P/E ~68-70x and EV/EBITDA ~38.9x versus listed peers Narayana Hrudayalaya (~27.5x) and Global Health (~33x) on EV/EBITDA. Sell-side (SMIFS TP Rs 1,130; ICICI Securities TP Rs 1,050) gets there by capitalizing the hospital business at 25-30x FY28E EV/EBITDA and diagnostics at 22-23x -- i.e. the Street's own bull case already sits at the top of the historical multiple range, not a margin of safety below it. My read: the current price is pricing sustained mid-to-high-teens revenue growth AND full, on-schedule delivery of the 25% EBITDA-margin target with no execution slip -- a 'clear the bar exactly, no more' valuation. The 9.25% trailing ROE (depressed by M&A goodwill and by newly added/acquired beds that have not yet matured to full ARPOB) is the tell that the earnings power being capitalized doesn't fully exist yet -- this is a forward capacity-ramp and margin story, not a value case on today's numbers. Verdict: rich, not cheap.

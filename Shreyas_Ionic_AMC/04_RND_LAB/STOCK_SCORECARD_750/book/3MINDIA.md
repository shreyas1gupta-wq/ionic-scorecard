## Detailed rationale

3M India (75% held by 3M Company, USA; NSE-listed since 2004) is a diversified MNC subsidiary across four segments: Transportation & Electronics (36% of FY26 sales, Rs 1,846cr, +10.1%), Safety & Industrial (32%, Rs 1,640cr, +16%), Health Care (20%, Rs 1,017cr, +17.5%) and Consumer (11%, Rs 537cr, +15.6%). FY26 (year to Mar-2026) revenue was Rs 5,089.8cr (+14% YoY), PBT Rs 894cr (+16%), but net profit only Rs 522.3cr (+9.7%) - the PBT/PAT growth gap reflects a higher effective tax rate this year tied to an Advance Pricing Agreement tax/interest recognition. Business quality is genuinely strong: debt-free, ROE ~30%, ROCE ~50%, oligopolistic niche categories (abrasives, PPE, healthcare tapes/wound-care, electrical/auto components) with pricing power and access to the global parent's product pipeline; manufacturing at Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Pune plus a Bengaluru R&D centre.

Q4 FY26 (Mar-2026 quarter) revenue was Rs 1,399.2cr (+17% YoY) and PAT Rs 215.3cr, a headline +202% YoY jump - but this is a base-effect illusion: the year-ago quarter (Q4 FY25) printed a weak Rs 71.4cr on its own exceptional charge. Ex-exceptionals, Q4 FY26 PBT was Rs 248.9cr, +10.5% YoY - a far more honest read of underlying momentum than the 202% headline. [DATA/INFERENCE] Earnings-quality flag: this is now the second of the last three quarters carrying a 'exceptional' labour-code-transition charge - Q3 FY26 (Dec-2025 quarter) took a Rs 74.6cr exceptional hit (swinging that quarter to a net loss) tied to India's new Labour Codes effective 21-Nov-2025, and Q4 FY26 took a further Rs 40.2cr charge from the same transition. Recurring 'exceptional' items across consecutive quarters is a mild red flag on earnings quality/comparability even though the underlying cause (one-time labour-code compliance cost) is legitimate and should taper by FY27.

Valuation is the central issue. Market cap is ~Rs 39,500-39,900cr against Rs 522cr FY26 PAT: trailing PE ~72-76x, forward PE ~49x on FY27 consensus, ~22x book value. Growth actually delivered has been far more modest than the multiple implies: 3-year profit CAGR is just 9%, 10-year profit CAGR 11%, 10-year sales CAGR 9% (5-year profit CAGR of 30% is base-effect-inflated off a depressed FY22). Promoter holds 75% with zero pledge (clean, no legal-status concern - 3M Company USA is the promoter, not a domestic group with legal overhang), but public float is only ~13%, which mechanically thins liquidity and inflates the observed multiple - a scarcity premium, not a cash-flow-justified one. Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026 quarter) results are NOT yet out; board meeting to approve them is scheduled 14-Aug-2026 (trading window closed 1-Jul to 16-Aug-2026).

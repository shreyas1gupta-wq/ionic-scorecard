# D-034 (2026-07-13)

Principal: **PORTFOLIO-LEVEL ADJUDICATION FOR SLEEVES.** "Don't be too strict — if a strategy is good we can use it; individual strategies can have >25% MDD and a little less CAGR/Sharpe but higher XIRR or portfolio use or regime-specific value." Standalone bars on a frozen card still bind THAT card's verdict (no retro-editing), but a standalone-bar FAIL does NOT bin a sleeve with genuine placebo-beating alpha: the deciding test is marginal contribution at BOOK level (weighted stack CAGR/DD/Sharpe, XIRR, regime-conditional value). Book-level risk bars (RISK_LIMITS) remain the binding constraint. First application: CA (+14.1% CAGR, −50% standalone DD, beats placebo95 8.9%) stays live as a book-sleeve candidate despite CA-COLLAR NOT-ARMORED.

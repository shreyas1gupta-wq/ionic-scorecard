How scoring works & how to use catalysts

PEAD Score (0–100, auto from fundamentals): Growth magnitude (30) — YoY on PAT/Sales/OP, scaled for fly-worthy prints, not 15% hurdles. Acceleration (20) — QoQ momentum + sequential level breaking trend. Margin / operating leverage (25) — OPM expansion + OP growing faster than sales. Quality of beat (25) — starts full, docked for PAT growing far faster than OP (other-income/tax/one-off tell), sales down while profit up, low absolute base (%-illusions), and loss quarters. This is the durability filter the raw screener can't give you.

Catalyst stickiness: for each identified name, log the real drivers (order win, capacity ramp, margin reset, price hike, new product, guidance raise, restructuring, sector upcycle, or one-off). Rate each by how long it persists — Structural (multi-year) / Multi-quarter / 1–2 quarters / One-off. Drift lives on durable catalysts that force multi-quarter estimate upgrades; a one-off beat rarely drifts. The tracker surfaces the most durable catalyst as the stickiness grade.

You handle the tape: log the daily close/high/low/volume to watch the base build; that view is for your eyes, not scored.

Data: Tijori public quarterly-results feed (fundamentals only). No price/volume — that's your read.

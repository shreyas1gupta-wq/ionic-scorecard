## S-07 — PEAD Q5 (Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift), equity long-only (BACKTEST STAGE, 2026-07-14)
- **Signal:** rank all earnings events each quarter by the stock's price reaction on the first trading day at/after `available_date` (strict PIT, no lookahead on quarter-end). Top quintile (Q5) only — the effect is NOT symmetric: Q1 (worst reaction) shows no negative drift, so a long-short version adds nothing.
- **Entry:** T+2 trading days after the reaction bar, at open + 0.15% slip (avoids paying into the event-day spike itself). Entry-day gap risk checked and clean: mean +0.39%, only 0.6% of entries gap ≥5%.
- **Exit:** 60 trading-day time exit, **NO stop-loss, NO trail** — every SL (fixed 10/15%, swing-low) and every trail (EMA20, 3-day-below) tested strictly WORSE (Sharpe drops to 0.7-1.0, win rate collapses ~59%→33%). Same monotone "wide stop + full hold wins" law found on the Chartlink scanner, now cross-validated on a second, independent signal family.
- **Sizing:** 5-8.5% of NAV per position, no leverage, cash-constrained (skip if full), 20%→15% drift trim, CA-guard (exit-day open gap ≤-25% = corporate action, not real price, exit at prior close). Sizing sweep peaks at 8.5% (CAGR 33.7%, Sharpe 1.58); >10% degrades on over-concentration.
- **What did NOT improve it (tested, rejected):** fundamental earnings-surprise MAGNITUDE (np_yoy growth) stacked on top — worse, not better (market reaction matters, not accounting-beat size); 52-week-high proximity stacked — worse CAGR (fewer trades); conviction-weighted sizing by RS/momentum — worse than plain equal-weight at every level tested. Lesson: additional stacking/filtering keeps reducing portfolio breadth faster than it improves per-trade quality.
- **Diversification value:** positive in 2022 (+10.6% to +16.9% depending on config) — the one year the Chartlink/gap-momentum book was hurt. Genuinely different risk exposure (earnings-event driven, not price-breakout driven).
- **Known caveats (not yet resolved):** gross returns, not beta/sector-adjusted — some edge may be high-beta names riding broad rallies rather than pure alpha; hasn't had Red Team / Gate-4 sensitivity / DSR-PBO treatment yet; 2025 weak (-9.4%) matching the broader session-wide pattern that 2025 was hard across styles; STCG tax not modeled.

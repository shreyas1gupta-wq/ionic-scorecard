## THE 2010-2018 GAP

**Problem:** NSE's official earnings-results archives (nsearchives) only cover 2019 onward. There is no free, documented Indian quarterly-earnings-with-announcement-dates source for 2010-2018 discovered.

**Fallback Options (in priority order):**

1. **Use board_meetings_all.json (ROUTE 3, secondary audit pending):** Board meetings flagged "Financial Results" ARE the announcements. May extend 2010-2018 coverage if NSE's board-meeting history goes further back than quarterly-results. *Action: Quick audit of earliest date in board_meetings_all.json.*

2. **Manual SEBI regulatory-deadline proxy (conservative, PIT-safe):** For any symbol-quarter missing from NSE archives, use rule:  
   `available_date_reconstruction = quarter_end_date + 45 days` (SEBI deadline for quarterly results publication per Rule 33 LODR). Flag as `available_date_recon` to distinguish from exact dates. This is conservative-late (safe) but loses announcement precision.

3. **Acknowledge gap & restrict validation to 2019+:** Accept 7-year horizon (2019-2026) for PIT-safe testing; note in every catalog entry that pre-2019 fundamentals analysis is not PIT-validated.

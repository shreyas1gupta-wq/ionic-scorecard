# MULTIBAGGER DNA — cross-era synthesis (2007-2025), actionable
Synthesis of 4 parallel era studies (sourced) + the quantitative dump. The repeatable
patterns that produced India's biggest winners, and the rules they imply for the engine.

## THREE ARCHETYPES (every multibagger is one of these — tag them)
1. **QUALITY COMPOUNDER (durable — HOLD long):** high RoE, low debt, secular demand,
   brand/moat. Bought at the intersection of an EARNINGS INFLECTION + a multi-year base
   breakout, while still cheap. Page, Titan, TTK ('10-11); PI, Astral, Ajanta, KPR ('12-16);
   Bajaj Fin, Bata ('18-19); Laurus, SRF, Dixon ('20); BSE, KFINTECH, Muthoot ('23-25).
   → These keep making new highs for years. The core of the book.
2. **RERATING / TURNAROUND (off a washed-out base — ride then EXIT when rerating done):**
   deep cyclical/PSU/defence/turnaround re-rating from a decade-low base + a REAL catalyst
   (order book, govt capex, ownership reset, balance-sheet repair). Tata Motors, Aurobindo
   ('09); CG Power ('20, only AFTER Murugappa recap); PSU/defence/railways/power ('22-23);
   Suzlon. → Biggest absolute % gains often come from HERE, not from high-RS leaders.
3. **JUNK / LIQUIDITY (rented gains — AVOID or trade-and-exit-fast):** leverage + narrative
   + low float + a liquidity mania. 2007 infra/realty/SEZ (Jai Corp, Punj Lloyd → ~total
   loss); 2017 smallcap mania (Vakrangee → −62% in 9 days); 2024-25 smallcap froth.
   → Explosive but fragile; gives it all back. Tight trail, small size, or skip.

## THE UNIVERSAL WINNING SETUP (consistent across ALL eras)
- **CHART:** multi-year (often decade-long) BASE → high-volume STAGE-2 BREAKOUT to new
  highs. NOT a parabolic blow-off (=junk top), NOT bottom-fishing a downtrend. Turnarounds
  break out of a depressed base on the fundamental-turn quarter. (Quant dump confirms: 100%
  of >=2x winners passed the trend-template in-year; the entry is the breakout.)
- **CATALYST (the timing trigger):** an EARNINGS INFLECTION (margin/loss-to-profit/order
  book) or a STRUCTURAL/POLICY catalyst (PLI, China+1, defence indigenisation, govt capex,
  ownership reset). Theme + earnings = stays; theme alone = trade.
- **FUNDAMENTALS:** durable winners had high RoE + low debt + real cash earnings. Leverage +
  cyclicality = rented gains (every permanent wealth-destroyer of the era was levered/cyclical).
- **REGIME:** multibaggers CLUSTER in GREEN breadth years (2009:159 2x, 2014:86, 2007:93,
  2021:54, 2023:42) and vanish in RED (2008:1, 2011:0, 2018:0, 2019:0). Be aggressive only
  when breadth supports it (validates the regime filter).

## THE BIGGEST CROSS-ERA LESSONS
1. **Follow the government's wallet.** Every durable theme mapped to a policy lever —
   2014 capex-hope, 2020 PLI/China+1, 2022-23 defence/railways/PSU-recap/power. Policy
   creates the VISIBLE multi-year order book that justifies a re-rating off a cheap base.
2. **Rerating off a CHEAP base = durable; off an EXPENSIVE base = the crash.** The same
   sector leadership that compounds early becomes the blow-up late (2007, 2018, 2025 smallcaps).
3. **"Picks-and-shovels" beat the boom.** BSE/KFINTECH/CDSL monetised the retail-investing
   boom regardless of which stock won; EMS monetised electronics regardless of brand. Highest
   quality-to-risk.
4. **Flows are accelerant AND poison.** When breadth is INDISCRIMINATE (junk ripping with
   quality) and inflows run multiples of normal → late-cycle: size down, demand earnings.
5. **The biggest % winners come from maximum-pessimism + a real catalyst, not maximum
   euphoria** (2009 turnarounds > 2007 mania). Many top winners START at LOW RS (turnarounds);
   our quant dump confirms (CENTURYPLY rs1, WHIRLPOOL rs2, CGPOWER rs1).

## RULE CHANGES for the engine (fold into PLAN)
- **Quality-momentum overlay:** gate/boost momentum picks by RoE + low-debt + earnings-
  acceleration (needs fundamentals data → fetch). Would catch durable winners, drop junk
  that round-trips. HIGH PRIORITY.
- **Sector-momentum tilt:** overweight the year's hot structural/policy theme sectors
  (needs a sector map → fetch). Multibaggers cluster in the leading sector each year.
- **Two archetype tracks:** a "compounder" track (hold through 23% heat, wide trail) + a
  "rerating/turnaround" track (catch low-RS names breaking out of decade bases on a catalyst).
- **Froth/regime-exit detector:** indiscriminate breadth + stretched valuations + junk
  leadership + regulatory pins (ASM/SEBI curbs) + credit events → de-risk to cash.
- **Entry = 52w-high base breakout + volume confirmation** (add volume when data available).

- **Follow the government's wallet**: every durable theme = a policy lever (capex, PLI/ECMS/ISM, defence indigenisation, EPR, grid capex, financialization). One macro engine: capex + manufacturing + financialization + energy-transition.
- **"Horizontal picks-and-shovels" beat the headline**: water, recycling, transmission/cables, DC-power-suppliers (not fibre) — monetize the trend regardless of which frontline name wins.

- **Most pure component/recycling/water plays are SMALL/illiquid or unlisted** → capacity-limited = fits ≤10Cr; but demands liquidity discipline + quality/governance screen (fraud tail).

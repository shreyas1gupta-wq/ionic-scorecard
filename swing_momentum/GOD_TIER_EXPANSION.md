## THE GOD-TIER PHILOSOPHY (what apex small-capital traders actually do)
The greats compounded small→large NOT via one signal but via:
1. **Capacity-as-moat** — fish where institutions CAN'T (microcaps, special-sits, thin
   options). At ≤₹10Cr these inefficiencies are huge and persistent. Size is the edge.
2. **Asymmetry** — only take convex bets: capped downside (hard stop / defined-risk),
   uncapped upside. Refuse symmetric gambles. (Soros: "It's not whether you're right,
   it's how much when right vs wrong.")
3. **Concentration + conviction** — a few highest-edge bets sized big, not 50 diworsified
   positions. Geometric compounding rewards concentration IF risk is capped (Kelly).
4. **Multi-strategy, low correlation** — many small uncorrelated edges (momentum +
   event-driven + special-sits + mean-reversion + flow) → smooth √N compounding.
5. **Ride structural tailwinds** — 2026-2040 India is a generational deepening story;
   position in the multi-year megatrends, not just the next breakout.
6. **The operation is an alpha-DISCOVERY machine** — perpetually find/validate/retire
   edges (Track3 loop). Edges decay; the meta-edge is renewal.
HONEST: "huge alpha" = compounding many capped-risk asymmetric small edges with elite
risk control — NOT leverage or a magic signal. Discipline > genius.

### D1 — SPECIAL SITUATIONS / event-driven ★ top add, uncorrelated to momentum
- Merger/acquisition arb (open-offer spread), demerger/spin-off value-unlock, buyback &
  tender-offer arb, rights-issue, delisting reverse-book-building, scheme-of-arrangement.
- Why small: spreads are tiny in ₹ terms → institutions skip; ≤₹10Cr captures them.
- Why uncorrelated: payoff driven by DEAL outcome/timeline, not market beta.
- Test: build India corporate-action/deal calendar; backtest open-offer & buyback spreads
  vs completion; downside = deal-break (define & cap). Convex where downside floored.

### D2 — IPO ECOSYSTEM alpha ★ India boom, highly capacity-limited

  fast-track inclusion flows.
- Why small: allocations & first-day liquidity are tiny → only small capital plays it.
- Test: IPO database (subscription, GMP, anchors, lockup dates) → listing & post-lockup
  return studies. (Mirror of the options project's post-IPO skills — reuse that thinking.)

### D3 — MICROCAP / SME quality-momentum ★ the purest capacity moat

- Test: microcap universe + CANSLIM-style fundamental accel + RS + liquidity-growth;

### D4 — POST-EARNINGS DRIFT (PEAD) + guidance-revision ★ robust, persistent anomaly

- Why uncorrelated: event-clock, not market-clock. Test: surprise → forward 1-12w return.

### D5 — THEMATIC / megatrend rotation (multi-year compounding)

### D6 — PAIRS / market-neutral relative value (beta-reducer)

### D7 — SEASONALITY / calendar & structural-flow anomalies

### D8 — CONVEXITY / catalyst options (defined-risk asymmetry)
- Buy cheap defined-risk options/spreads INTO known catalysts (results, events, breakouts)

### D9 — INSIDER / PROMOTER / smart-money following

# /board-meet — monthly board (Principal chairs; protocol: 08_BOARD_ROOM/BOARD_CHARTER.md)

1. **Assemble the pre-read pack first** (mechanical, cheap tier): fill `08_BOARD_ROOM/month_end/YYYY-MM_checkpoint.md` from the charter's template — plan-vs-actual (QUARTERLY_PLAN milestones), book states (STRATEGY_REGISTER), pipeline moves (IDEA_PIPELINE + KILLED_IDEAS deltas), paper P&L + TE (PAPER_LEDGER), risk standing-order compliance, data health (catalog changes), AP league + coaching (LEADERBOARD), token spend (WORK_LOG).
2. **Reports (parallel, tight):** spawn cio-rajan-mehta (firm view + tail posture, 5 lines) and the three FMs (book report, 5 lines each: moved/died/asking). Data Officer 3-liner if data changed materially.
3. **Decisions-only agenda:** list ONLY items needing a ruling (Principal or CIO-delegated). Present to the Principal; record his rulings as D-series entries.
4. **File:** `minutes/YYYY-MM_board_minutes.md` (decisions, action items with owner+date, dissents) + the checkpoint's NEXT MONTH section (3-5 committed deliverables per book — next month's plan-vs-actual baseline).
5. **AP posting** + Analyst-of-the-Month. Journal one line; commit.
Quarterly boards add: plan refresh (CIO+FMs), /review-team output, process red-team.
